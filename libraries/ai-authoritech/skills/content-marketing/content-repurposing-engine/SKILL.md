# Content Repurposing Engine

Use the [operating standard](references/content-repurposing-standard.md) and [working template](assets/content-repurposing-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Identify authoritative source, audience, objective, rights, claims, key ideas, examples, and prohibited changes.
2. Select derivative formats and adapt structure, length, hook, context, call to action, accessibility, and channel conventions.
3. Trace each factual claim to the source, flag gaps, avoid false quotations, and create a reuse manifest.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, verified inputs, analysis, recommendations or deliverables, assumptions, owners, approvals, measurement, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and protect confidential client information.
- Do not invent performance, customers, quotations, research, rankings, or claims.
- Respect copyright, trademarks, privacy, permissions, and platform rules.
- Separate observation from inference and label assumptions.

## Recovery

If source rights, accuracy, freshness, confidentiality, citation, disclosure, or approval is unresolved, do not distribute the derivative content. Preserve the source and flag the affected passages for accountable review.
