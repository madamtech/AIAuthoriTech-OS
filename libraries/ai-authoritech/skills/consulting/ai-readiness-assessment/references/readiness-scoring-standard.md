# Readiness Scoring Standard

Score each supported criterion from 0 to 4: absent, initial, developing, managed,
or optimized. Do not score missing evidence. Record the source, date, confidence,
and contradiction status for every criterion.

Calculate the evidenced-weight score as:

`sum((criterion score / 4) * dimension weight) / evidenced weights * 100`

Report evidence coverage beside the score. A result below 60 percent coverage is
provisional. Critical blockers remain visible regardless of the average.

Use confidence labels high, medium, or low. High requires corroborated operating
evidence; medium may rely on one credible source; low relies on unverified claims
or indirect evidence.
