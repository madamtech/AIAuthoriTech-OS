# Knowledge governance standard

## Source admission

Require:

- legitimate purpose and ingestion authority
- accountable content owner
- identifiable system or document of record
- provenance and stable identifier
- audience and access classification
- effective date, version, status, and jurisdiction where relevant
- freshness expectation and update mechanism
- retention and deletion requirements
- extractable content of sufficient quality

## Authority hierarchy

Define precedence for each domain, for example:

1. controlling law or externally binding requirement
2. approved organizational policy
3. approved standard or procedure
4. system-of-record data
5. maintained guidance
6. historical or illustrative content

Do not assume this example fits every domain. Record conflicts, effective dates,
exceptions, and qualified-review needs.

## Evaluation measures

Measure retrieval relevance, authoritative-source selection, permission enforcement,
citation correctness, answer support, abstention quality, freshness, latency, and
cost. Evaluate end-to-end questions rather than chunk similarity alone.
