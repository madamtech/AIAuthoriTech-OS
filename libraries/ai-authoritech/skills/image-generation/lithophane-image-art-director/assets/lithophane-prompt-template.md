# Lithophane Image Prompt Template

Use this template to create new lithophane-ready source artwork.

## Master Prompt

Create a **high-resolution lithophane-optimized grayscale image** of [SUBJECT / SCENE].

Composition:
- [PRIMARY FOCAL SUBJECT]
- [SECONDARY OBJECTS]
- [BACKGROUND / TEXTURE]
- [TEXT, if any]
- [TARGET ASPECT RATIO OR DIMENSIONS]

Visual treatment:
- dark, detailed, sculpted grayscale aesthetic
- deep connected shadow masses in hair, folds, recesses, object shadows, and background depth
- protected bright highlights on focal features, eyes, pearls, gems, glass, chrome, or metallic edges
- rich separated midtones from deep charcoal through medium and light gray
- strong local contrast around every important subject and object
- crisp sculpted edges and clearly readable silhouettes
- directional dimensional lighting with visible highlight side and shadow side
- layered depth separation between foreground, main subject, secondary objects, and background
- dense but print-readable micro-detail
- embossed, beveled, engraved, metallic, jeweled, or sculptural surface detail when appropriate
- minimal flat empty areas
- strong focal-point hierarchy

Lithophane optimization:
**Lithophane-optimized high-resolution grayscale with deep connected shadow masses, protected bright highlights, rich separated midtones, strong local contrast, crisp sculpted edges, directional dimensional lighting, print-readable micro-detail, and clearly separated foreground, subject, and background depth.**

Tonal control:
**Preserve deep blacks, luminous highlights, and smooth tonal gradients without muddy gray blending. Avoid washed-out tones, weak outlines, blown-out whites, crushed featureless blacks, low-contrast adjacent objects, excessive flat empty areas, and tiny details that will disappear in 3D printing.**

Resolution and file quality:
- generate at the highest practical native resolution
- preserve the final aspect ratio from generation
- target 3000+ px on the long edge when supported
- prefer 4K-class output or larger for complex artwork when available
- PNG / lossless output preferred
- avoid JPEG compression artifacts

If the image is intended for a lampshade or lightbox maker that accepts a normal image, output a **flat straight rectangular composition**. Do not add curved top/bottom edges unless explicitly required.

## Portrait Add-On
Add this when a person is present:

Maintain natural facial anatomy while strengthening lithophane readability. Clearly define eyes, lashes, brows, nostrils, lips, jawline, cheek shadows, hairline, and major hair waves. Keep dark hair detailed internally rather than turning it into a solid black block. Preserve selective facial highlights and dimensional cheek/nose modeling.

## Typography Add-On
Add this when text is present:

Use correctly spelled, readable typography with adequate stroke thickness, strong tonal separation from its background, crisp edges, and optional bevel/shadow/plaque treatment for dimensional separation. Avoid ultra-thin lettering that may disappear after 3D conversion.

## Luxury / Glam Add-On
Use when the desired aesthetic is similar to metallic grayscale glamour artwork:

Use a luxurious monochrome silver-and-charcoal visual language with tufted or quilted textures, polished chrome, engraved metal, pearls, faceted gems, jeweled accents, glossy glass, carved plaques, dramatic shadows, and bright specular highlights. Keep the focal subject dominant while decorative objects reinforce depth rather than compete with it.

## Revision Prompt: Too Light / Weak
Revise the existing image for stronger lithophane depth. Preserve the approved composition and subject. Deepen connected shadow masses, increase local contrast around focal features, strengthen edge definition, restore deep charcoal/black anchors, preserve bright highlight accents, enrich separated midtones, and reduce pale washed-out regions. Maintain high resolution and all approved details.

## Revision Prompt: Too Dark / Muddy
Revise the existing image while preserving composition and style. Open selected shadow regions to reveal internal texture, restore midtone separation, brighten protected highlights, separate overlapping dark objects, strengthen important facial features, and prevent large black regions from losing detail. Keep deep anchor shadows but avoid crushed blacks.

## Quick QA Checklist
Before accepting the image, confirm:
- dark anchors are present
- highlights are selective
- midtones are visibly separated
- focal features remain readable at thumbnail size
- adjacent objects do not merge tonally
- edges are crisp
- background is subordinate to the focal subject
- high-resolution output is used
- aspect ratio matches the final product
- no unnecessary curved silhouette is baked into a flat-source image
