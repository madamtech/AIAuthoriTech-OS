# Skincare Concept Standard

Define intended user, body area, product form, desired sensory profile, ingredient philosophy, allergens, contraindications, packaging, stability, preservation, labeling, and target market before proposing a concept. Separate cosmetic positioning from medical claims. Verify supplier documentation, usage limits, compatibility, sanitation, batch records, and applicable regulatory requirements. Require qualified formulation, stability, microbial, packaging, and safety review before sale or use.
