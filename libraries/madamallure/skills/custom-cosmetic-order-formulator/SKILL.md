# Custom Cosmetic Order Formulator

## Procedure

1. Capture the requested product, use site, age group, scent, texture, color,
   botanical preferences, exclusions, known sensitivities, quantity, deadline,
   packaging, budget, and consent to documented limitations.
2. Screen for medical, infant, pregnancy, broken-skin, allergy, eye-area, ingestion,
   or therapeutic requests and route them to qualified review or decline them.
3. Decide whether the request is an approved-base variation or a new formula.
4. Route formula work to the relevant specialist skill. Do not substitute oils,
   fragrance, preservatives, emulsifiers, alkali, or functional ingredients by analogy.
5. Recalculate the complete 100-percent formula, batch, fragrance limits, process,
   tests, label, price, and lead time for every material change.
6. Define a sample or bench-trial approval gate before production when the change
   affects formula behavior, scent, color, preservation, packaging, or claims.
7. Deliver with [assets/custom-formulation-order-brief.md](assets/custom-formulation-order-brief.md)
   and apply [references/custom-formulation-standard.md](references/custom-formulation-standard.md).

## Output Contract

Return the customer brief, base-or-new decision, specialist route, exclusions,
formula and test requirements, sample gate, packaging, labeling, price inputs,
lead-time assumptions, customer approvals, risks, and order status.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise hypoallergenic, irritation-free, pregnancy-safe, or medically suitable.
- Do not accept therapeutic claims or diagnose a customer's condition.
- Do not skip testing because the batch is small or made for one person.
- Do not reveal proprietary master formulas in customer-facing documents.
- Do not produce before customer, formula, pricing, and safety approvals are complete.

## Recovery

If the request is unsafe, medically framed, unsupported, or lacks essential supplier
evidence, decline or place it on hold with a clear reason. If the deadline cannot
accommodate required testing, offer an already-approved formula option rather than
skipping controls.
