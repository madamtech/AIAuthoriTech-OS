# Cinematic Story Standard

Develop story from purpose, audience, genre, theme, format, runtime, emotional arc, world rules, characters, stakes, conflict, turning points, ending, content boundaries, originality, rights, budget, and production constraints. Preserve causal coherence and visual storytelling while tracking assumptions and revisions. Avoid copying protected expression or representing invented facts as true. Test premise, structure, character motivation, pacing, tone, feasibility, and audience impact before approval.
