# Government Business Requirements Builder

## Procedure

1. Collect the business problem, mission context, stakeholders, desired outcomes, scope, constraints, policies supplied by the user, and success measures.
2. Organize business requirements separately from functional or technical design.
3. Return a traceable BRD with priorities, dependencies, assumptions, risks, and clarifications needed.

## Output Contract

Provide document scope, verified source basis, required sections, assumptions, clarifications needed, the requested functional or administrative deliverable, review owners, and validation requirements.

## Guardrails

- Do not convert policy into legal interpretation.
- Do not invent business rules, statutory requirements, stakeholder authority, or success metrics.
- Keep technical implementation outside the BRD unless included only as a clearly labeled external dependency.

## Recovery

If source evidence, authority, role ownership, process steps, required fields, or template requirements are incomplete, preserve verified content, label the gap, and request only the minimum clarification or authorized review required to continue.
