# Implementation standard

Use only applicable workstreams:

1. program governance and delivery management
2. business process and operating model
3. solution and integration architecture
4. data readiness, migration, and quality
5. AI or model design, evaluation, and lifecycle
6. application, automation, and infrastructure delivery
7. security, privacy, compliance, and responsible AI
8. testing and quality assurance
9. change, communications, training, and adoption
10. deployment, cutover, rollback, and continuity
11. operations, monitoring, support, and retirement
12. value measurement and benefits realization

## Gate contract

For every gate define:

- decision and accountable authority
- entry criteria and required evidence
- reviewers
- pass, conditional-pass, and fail outcomes
- remediation owner
- expiration or re-review trigger

Never make a gate ceremonial; it must be able to stop progression.
