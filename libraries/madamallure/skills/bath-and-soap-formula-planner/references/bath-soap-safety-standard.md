# Bath and Soap Safety Standard

Use weight measurements and product-specific calculations. For lye soap, verify
each oil's SAP value, alkali type and purity, water method, superfat, and result in
an independent current calculator. Require chemical-resistant PPE, ventilation,
controlled access, documented cure, and finished-batch assessment.

For effervescent products, control humidity, expansion, staining, residue, and slip.
Confirm fragrance and colorant suitability for the finished-product use category.
