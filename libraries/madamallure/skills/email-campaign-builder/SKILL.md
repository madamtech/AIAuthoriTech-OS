# Email Campaign Builder

Use the [operating standard](references/email-campaign-standard.md) and [working template](assets/email-campaign-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define objective, segment, consent basis, offer, schedule, brand voice, assets, landing pages, and success measures.
2. Create subject, preheader, message hierarchy, product modules, call to action, alt text, footer, and variants.
3. Test personalization, links, mobile rendering, exclusions, disclosures, send timing, and tracking before approval.

## Output Contract

Provide verified inputs, specifications, assumptions, risks, approvals, execution steps, owners, and validation criteria.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and label estimates.
- Protect customer, supplier, and proprietary information.
- Require approval before irreversible production, pricing, or customer communication.
- Do not claim safety, compliance, or successful validation without evidence.

## Recovery

If audience authority, suppression, claims, links, disclosures, unsubscribe, schedule, or owner approval is unresolved, keep the campaign unsent. Do not use unverified customer data or promises.
