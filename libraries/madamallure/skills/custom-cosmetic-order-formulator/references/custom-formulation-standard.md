# Custom Formulation Standard

Treat every material formula change as a controlled change. Classify the order as
an approved-base variation or new development. Reassess formula percentage, process,
fragrance limits, preservation, stability, packaging, labeling, cost, and lead time.

Capture customer preferences without treating health information as formulation
authority. Minimize sensitive data and never promise absence of allergy or irritation.
Keep proprietary formulas in internal records; provide customers only necessary
ingredient, use, warning, approval, and order information.
