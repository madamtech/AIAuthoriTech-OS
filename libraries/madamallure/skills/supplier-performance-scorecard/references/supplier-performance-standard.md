# Supplier Performance Standard

Score suppliers using comparable periods and verified purchase orders, acknowledgments, receipts, inspections, defects, certificates, lot traceability, responsiveness, lead times, price changes, service, sustainability, and corrective actions. Define metric formulas, weights, minimum sample size, data confidence, severity, and escalation thresholds before scoring. Separate objective facts from observations, protect confidential information, and require human review for qualification, suspension, or termination decisions.
