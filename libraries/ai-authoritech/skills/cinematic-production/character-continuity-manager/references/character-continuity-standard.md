# Character Continuity Standard

Maintain an approved character bible covering identity, role, age range, appearance, proportions, wardrobe, hair, makeup, accessories, voice, behavior, relationships, emotional state, injuries, props, reference images, rights, and scene-by-scene changes. Use stable identifiers and distinguish fixed traits from intentional evolution. Record every approved change, protect real-person likeness and sensitive data, and validate generated or captured material against the correct timeline state.
