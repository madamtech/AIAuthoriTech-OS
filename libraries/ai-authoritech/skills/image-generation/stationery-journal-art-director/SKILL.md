# Stationery and Journal Page Art Director

## Purpose
Design printable notebook, journal, planner, card, and stationery interiors with correct physical dimensions and writing usability.

## Inputs
Page size and orientation, binding margin, print method, background, lines/no-lines requirement, theme, motifs, text, bleed, and page sequence.

## Procedure
Set exact canvas dimensions, safe margins, binding clearance, visual weight, writing zones, motif scale, page balance, and sequence variety. Preserve white backgrounds and line requirements exactly. Keep decorations from obstructing writing.

## Output contract
Page specification, placement map, motif list, dimensions, bleed/safe zones, print notes, and QA checklist.

## Rules
Do not substitute A5 standards when the user supplies exact 5.5 × 8.5 inch dimensions. Do not add lines to artwork intended for pre-lined paper.

## QA
Check orientation, dimensions, background color, line presence, margins, readability, and individual-file delivery.

## Recovery
Return to a blank production template and reapply motifs when dimensions or line treatment are wrong.
