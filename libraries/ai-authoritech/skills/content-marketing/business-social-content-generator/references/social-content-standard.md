# Business Social Content Standard

Generate from a verified objective, audience, platform, campaign, approved claims, source facts, brand voice, format, accessibility, disclosure, rights, CTA, timing, moderation, and approval. Adapt to platform behavior without manufacturing urgency, results, endorsements, reviews, news, or trends. Protect confidential and personal information, label synthetic media when required, check links and rendering, and retain source-to-post traceability and publishing authority.
