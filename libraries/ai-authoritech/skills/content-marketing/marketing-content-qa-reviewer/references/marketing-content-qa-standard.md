# Marketing Content QA Standard

Review against the approved brief, audience, channel, source facts, claims evidence, citations, brand voice, offer terms, rights, disclosures, privacy, accessibility, links, personalization, formatting, technical constraints, and approval authority. Separate blocking defects from improvements and record exact evidence and location. Never silently rewrite approved meaning, certify legal compliance, or approve unsupported claims. Recheck corrected content and preserve the decision trail.
