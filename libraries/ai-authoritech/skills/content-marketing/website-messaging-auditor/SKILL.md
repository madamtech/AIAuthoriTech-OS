# Website Messaging Auditor

Use the [operating standard](references/website-messaging-audit-standard.md) and [working template](assets/website-messaging-audit-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define target audience, offer, page goals, traffic intent, desired actions, brand, and available evidence.
2. Review navigation, headlines, value, differentiation, proof, outcomes, process, objections, risk reversal, CTA, and cross-page consistency.
3. Prioritize findings by customer impact and effort, then propose evidence-aware replacement directions.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, verified inputs, analysis, recommendations or deliverables, assumptions, owners, approvals, measurement, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and protect confidential client information.
- Do not invent performance, customers, quotations, research, rankings, or claims.
- Respect copyright, trademarks, privacy, permissions, and platform rules.
- Separate observation from inference and label assumptions.

## Recovery

If the live page, target audience, offer, analytics context, or approved claims are unavailable, label the audit as provisional. Do not invent conversion results, customer motives, or competitor facts.
