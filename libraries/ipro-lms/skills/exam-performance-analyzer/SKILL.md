# Exam Performance Analyzer

Use statistical evidence as a review signal, not an automatic item-deletion rule.

## Workflow

1. Confirm exam form, blueprint, population, administration window, scoring, retake policy, sample size, and data quality.
2. Separate first attempts, retakes, incomplete attempts, forms, versions, and invalid administrations as required.
3. Calculate distribution, pass rate, central tendency, timing, item difficulty, discrimination, distractor selection, missing responses, and reliability where supportable.
4. Investigate anomalous items against objectives, source content, wording, key, delivery, and exposure evidence.
5. Examine subgroup differences carefully while protecting privacy and avoiding unsupported causal claims.
6. Recommend retain, review, revise, retire, or collect-more-data actions with evidence confidence.

## Output

Provide data scope, quality notes, exam metrics, item analysis, flagged patterns, security or fairness risks, recommendations, limitations, and monitoring plan.

## Guardrails

- Suppress secure item text and small sensitive cohorts.
- Do not change keys solely because of performance statistics.
- State sample-size and retake limitations.
- Require qualified review for psychometric claims.
