# Certification Health Monitor

Use the [operating standard](references/certification-monitoring-standard.md) and [working template](assets/certification-health-template.md).

Create an evidence-based operating review tied to owners and corrective actions.

## Procedure

1. Confirm certification goals, policies, owners, populations, period, systems, service levels, and risk thresholds.
2. Collect candidate, attempt, pass, issuance, renewal, expiration, reinstatement, notification, support, and exception measures.
3. Reconcile key totals and verify date, status, version, and population definitions.
4. Compare trends and thresholds while separating policy changes, seasonality, data defects, and operational causes.
5. Identify blocked candidates, delayed records, duplicate credentials, missed notices, unusual pass patterns, and renewal leakage.
6. Assign actions, owners, dates, evidence requirements, and escalation paths.

## Output Contract

Provide a health summary, KPI definitions, reconciled trends, exception register, risk assessment, action plan, decisions required, and next-review schedule.

## Guardrails

- Do not equate pass rate alone with program quality.
- Protect candidate and assessment data.
- Separate observed evidence from hypotheses.
- Never change credential status through monitoring alone.

## Recovery

If metric definitions, population, credential version, lifecycle rules, source reconciliation, alert threshold, or owner response is unresolved, issue a provisional alert only. Preserve records and require credential-owner approval for status or policy changes.
