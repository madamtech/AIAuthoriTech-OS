# Customer Review Analyzer

## Workflow

1. Define channels, period, products, populations, privacy limits, and business questions.
2. Normalize feedback and code sentiment, themes, severity, frequency, journey stage, and verified order context.
3. Separate anecdotes from patterns, identify expectation gaps and root-cause hypotheses, and prioritize actions with owners.

## Output

Provide verified inputs, specifications, assumptions, risks, approvals, owners, implementation actions, and validation criteria.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and label assumptions.
- Protect customer, supplier, formula, and commercial information.
- Do not make unsupported cosmetic, health, safety, compliance, or financial claims.
- Require qualified review and authorization before regulated, production, pricing, or customer-impacting action.
