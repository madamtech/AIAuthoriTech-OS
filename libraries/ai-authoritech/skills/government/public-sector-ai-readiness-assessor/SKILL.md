# Public Sector AI Readiness Assessor

Use the [public-sector readiness standard](references/public-sector-readiness-standard.md) and record evidence and decisions in the [public-sector readiness report template](assets/public-sector-readiness-report-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define agency scope, mission outcomes, authorities, stakeholders, services, constraints, and evidence standard.
2. Assess each readiness domain using documented evidence and confidence ratings.
3. Identify blockers, suitable starting points, dependencies, decisions, and a phased readiness roadmap.

4. Evaluate mission fit, service impact, rights, records, procurement, vendor, accessibility, security, workforce, oversight, redress, continuity, and measurement.
5. Separate agency-wide readiness from use-case readiness and apply hard gates to prohibited or unsupported uses.
6. Validate findings with accountable officials and affected stakeholder perspectives without claiming consensus.

## Output Contract

Deliver scope, authority, domain findings, evidence confidence, blockers, suitable starting points, risks, public-impact considerations, procurement and governance dependencies, phased roadmap, owners, decisions, and monitoring requirements.

## Guardrails

- Verify current jurisdiction, policy, legal, procurement, records, privacy, security, and accessibility requirements with qualified officials.
- Separate evidence from assumptions and label confidence.
- Protect sensitive, personal, procurement, security, and deliberative information.
- Do not claim compliance, authorization, public consensus, or completed government action without evidence.

## Recovery

If authority, legal or policy interpretation, protected-data handling, procurement status, security boundary, or public-impact evidence is unresolved, stop the affected recommendation and label it not assessed or conditional. Preserve protected information, route questions to qualified officials, and do not advance deployment or procurement.
