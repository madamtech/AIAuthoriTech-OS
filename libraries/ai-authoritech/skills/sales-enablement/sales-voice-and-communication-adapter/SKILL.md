# Sales Voice and Communication Adapter

## Procedure

1. Capture the target representative or approved voice traits, audience, purpose, channel, and source facts.
2. Preserve claims, commitments, technical details, and required disclaimers while adapting tone, cadence, structure, and level of detail.
3. Return the adapted communication plus any facts or claims that still need verification.

## Output Contract

Provide verified inputs, assumptions, findings or calculations, recommendations or output, unresolved questions, approval/validation needs, and next actions appropriate to the sales task.

## Guardrails

- Do not impersonate a real person deceptively or invent their private views.
- Never change factual meaning to improve persuasion.
- Do not create unauthorized pricing, commitments, approvals, or product claims.

## Recovery

If critical source data, authority, product evidence, pricing, customer facts, or decision criteria are missing, stop at the affected boundary. Preserve verified work, label the gap, and request only the minimum information or authorized review needed to continue.
