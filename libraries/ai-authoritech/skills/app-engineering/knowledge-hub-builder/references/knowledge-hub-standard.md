# Knowledge Hub Standard

## Content contract

Each item needs a stable ID, title, content type, audience, task, summary, owner,
authority, source, status, version, effective date, next review, locale,
sensitivity, permissions, rights, taxonomy, related items, feedback route, and
retention or deletion rule.

## Publishing lifecycle

Define transition authority and evidence for draft, review, approved, scheduled,
published, superseded, expired, archived, withdrawn, and deleted. Require
specialist review for regulated, safety-critical, legal, security, privacy, or
accessibility-sensitive material. Preserve redirects and supersession links.

## Search relevance

Build representative query sets from user tasks, vocabulary, misspellings,
abbreviations, exact identifiers, ambiguous terms, and zero-result cases.
Evaluate relevance, authority, freshness, permission correctness, snippets,
facets, latency, and task completion. Diagnose content gaps separately from search
configuration defects.

## AI answers

Filter authorization before retrieval. Preserve source title, stable ID, version,
effective date, section, and accessible link with every cited claim. Disclose
conflicts, uncertainty, and stale sources. Abstain when evidence is missing,
unauthorized, or insufficient. Evaluate ordinary, ambiguous, conflicting, stale,
restricted, adversarial, and no-answer queries.

## Freshness and quality

Set review cadence by risk and volatility. Monitor overdue review, failed
ingestion, missing owners, broken links, orphan content, duplicate authority,
permission drift, localization lag, accessibility defects, zero results, and
unresolved feedback.

## Verification

Test publishing transitions, permission changes, search and facets, effective
dates, supersession, redirects, multilingual routing, responsive layouts,
keyboard and assistive technology, attachments, imports, exports, AI citations,
abstention, deletion propagation, analytics privacy, and legacy cutover.
