# Certification Program Designer

Turn an approved business need into a defensible certification specification.

## Workflow

1. Define the credential purpose, population, business owner, scope, and claims a certified person may make.
2. Establish competencies and map them to learning and assessment evidence.
3. Define eligibility, prerequisites, delivery, accommodations, and identity requirements.
4. Specify assessment blueprint, passing standard, attempt limits, waiting periods, and remediation.
5. Define issuance, validity, expiration, renewal, grace, revocation, and reinstatement rules.
6. Document security, record retention, audit evidence, reporting, and approval responsibilities.
7. Test the design for ambiguity, unfairness, operational gaps, and unsupported platform assumptions.

## Output

Provide a program charter, competency matrix, candidate journey, assessment policy, lifecycle rules, governance model, LMS requirements, risk register, decisions needing approval, and implementation handoff.

## Guardrails

- Never invent legal, regulatory, HR, or accreditation requirements.
- Separate program policy from platform configuration.
- Make all date, attempt, and exception rules explicit.
- Require named approval for passing standards, credential claims, and revocation policy.
