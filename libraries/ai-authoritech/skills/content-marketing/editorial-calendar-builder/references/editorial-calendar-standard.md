# Editorial Calendar Standard

Build the calendar from strategy, audience journeys, campaigns, launches, seasonality, themes, formats, channels, owners, capacity, dependencies, source availability, rights, claims review, production lead times, accessibility, approvals, distribution, measurement, and reuse. Balance consistency with realistic throughput and content freshness. Define statuses, gates, buffers, substitutions, escalation, and change control so dates are commitments only when inputs and ownership are confirmed.
