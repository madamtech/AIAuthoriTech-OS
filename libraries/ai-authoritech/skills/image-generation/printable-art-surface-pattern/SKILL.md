# Printable Artwork and Surface Pattern Designer

Use the [operating standard](references/operating-standard.md) and [working template](assets/working-record.md).

## Purpose
Create artwork and repeat patterns suitable for physical printing, sublimation, vinyl, laser workflows, packaging, and 3D surface decoration.

## Inputs
Physical size, DPI, color limit, print method, substrate, bleed, safe area, transparency, repeat type, and brand rules.

## Procedure
Choose tile structure, motif scale, spacing, edge continuity, color separations, contrast, negative space, and substrate interaction. Build full-bleed or transparent outputs intentionally. Define vectorization needs and production tolerances.

## Output Contract
Pattern/art brief, repeat specification, dimensions, resolution, color profile/count, bleed/safe area, transparent-background rules, and production QA.

## Guardrails
Do not add lines, backgrounds, or decorative elements the user explicitly removed. Do not claim raster art is a true SVG.

## QA
Check seamless edges, physical-scale legibility, color count, transparency, bleed, and absence of unintended artifacts.

## Recovery
Test a small repeat tile and physical-size proof before scaling to production.
