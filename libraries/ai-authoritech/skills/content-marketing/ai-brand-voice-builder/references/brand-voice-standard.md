# AI Brand Voice Standard

Derive voice from approved brand strategy, audience, values, positioning, representative examples, exclusions, channel contexts, accessibility, claims, and legal constraints. Separate voice from tone and define controllable dimensions, vocabulary, sentence patterns, examples, counterexamples, sensitive topics, escalation, and review. Do not imitate a living creator, infer unsupported identity traits, or encode stereotypes. Test the guide across realistic content types and edge cases.
