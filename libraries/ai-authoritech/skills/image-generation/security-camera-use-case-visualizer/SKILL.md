# Security Camera Use-Case Visualizer

## Purpose
Place security cameras and related technology in believable customer environments so benefits and coverage are visually understandable.

## Inputs
Exact camera reference, premises images or description, mounting limitations, target area, business benefit, field-of-view intent, and brand rules.

## Procedure
Analyze mounting surface, height, angle, line of sight, occlusion, lighting, weather exposure, vandal risk, and customer workflow. Place the exact camera visibly but naturally. Show the operational benefit through scene context rather than fantasy overlays unless requested.

## Output contract
Placement rationale, scene prompt, mounting and orientation notes, benefit callouts, assumptions, and technical accuracy checklist.

## Rules
Do not change the camera model, housing, logo, lens arrangement, or mounting hardware. Do not claim measured coverage without validated specifications.

## QA
Check scale, mounting physics, sight line, environment usefulness, and product fidelity.

## Recovery
If premises details are insufficient, provide a clearly marked concept and list the site information needed for a validated demo.
