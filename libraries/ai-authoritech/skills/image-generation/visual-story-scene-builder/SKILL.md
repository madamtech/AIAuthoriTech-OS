# Visual Story Scene Builder

Use the [operating standard](references/operating-standard.md) and [working template](assets/working-record.md).

## Purpose
Convert a message or narrative beat into a visually readable scene with clear action, stakes, setting, and emotion.

## Inputs
Narrative objective, characters, location, time, action, emotional beat, props, continuity state, and output format.

## Procedure
Define what happened immediately before, what is happening now, and what the image must imply next. Select the decisive moment, blocking, eye lines, gesture, environmental evidence, foreground framing, and symbolic details. Keep every object narratively justified.

## Output Contract
Scene brief, decisive moment, character blocking, environment details, prop logic, emotional direction, composition, and continuity notes.

## Guardrails
Do not create a generic pose when an action can communicate the message. Avoid contradictory eye lines or physically impossible interactions.

## QA
A viewer should understand who, where, what, and emotional intent without a caption. Confirm continuity with previous scenes.

## Recovery
If the scene is confusing, simplify to one primary action and one supporting visual clue.
