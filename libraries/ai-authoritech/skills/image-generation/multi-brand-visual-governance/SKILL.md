# Multi-Brand Visual Governance

## Purpose
Prevent visual contamination across AI AuthoriTech, i-PRO, MadamAllure, client brands, and personal projects while allowing approved shared systems.

## Inputs
Active brand, participating brands, ownership of assets, shared colors/styles, campaign context, and reuse permissions.

## Procedure
Create a brand boundary matrix covering logo, palette, typography, character, tone, materials, motifs, and audience. Mark each element as exclusive, shared, adaptable, or prohibited. Resolve which brand leads and how secondary brands are credited. Route conflicts for approval.

## Output contract
Brand boundary matrix, lead-brand decision, permitted shared elements, prohibited transfers, attribution rules, and QA gates.

## Rules
No silent reuse of client or employer assets. i-PRO technical visuals must not inherit luxury MadamAllure styling unless explicitly requested for a concept. AI AuthoriTech colors are teal, metallic rose gold, shimmer/chrome sapphire blue, black, and white when that brand is active.

## QA
Review every visible brand signal and metadata field. Confirm that source ownership and reuse rights are known.

## Recovery
Remove disputed elements, return to neutral visual language, and rebuild only from approved brand assets.
