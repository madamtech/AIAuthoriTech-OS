# Customer Retention Campaign Planner

Use the [operating standard](references/retention-campaign-standard.md) and [working template](assets/retention-campaign-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define retention objective, eligible audience, consent, exclusions, lifecycle stage, value proposition, budget, and cadence.
2. Design segment-specific education, replenishment, loyalty, cross-sell, win-back, or appreciation journeys.
3. Set suppression, frequency, offer economics, tests, metrics, stop conditions, and customer-care readiness.

## Output Contract

Provide verified inputs, specifications, assumptions, risks, approvals, owners, implementation actions, and validation criteria.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and label assumptions.
- Protect customer, supplier, formula, and commercial information.
- Do not make unsupported cosmetic, health, safety, compliance, or financial claims.
- Require qualified review and authorization before regulated, production, pricing, or customer-impacting action.

## Recovery

If consent, suppression, segment logic, offer margin, inventory, claims, personalization, or approval is unresolved, do not activate the campaign. Preserve normal customer service and exclude ambiguous records until validated.
