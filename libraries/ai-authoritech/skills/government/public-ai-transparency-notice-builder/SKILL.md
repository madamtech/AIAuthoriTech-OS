# Public AI Transparency Notice Builder

Use the [transparency notice standard](references/transparency-notice-standard.md) and the [transparency notice template](assets/transparency-notice-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Confirm facts, audience, language, channel, accessibility, disclosure authority, security constraints, and responsible office.
2. Explain what the system does and does not do, when it is used, data categories, human involvement, limitations, safeguards, retention, and challenge routes.
3. Test accuracy, plain language, translation, accessibility, consistency with operations, and update ownership.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, authority, evidence, assumptions, findings, risks, controls, owners, decisions, and validation or monitoring requirements.

## Guardrails

- Verify current jurisdiction, policy, legal, records, procurement, privacy, security, and accessibility requirements with qualified officials.
- Protect sensitive, personal, security, vendor, and deliberative information.
- Separate observed evidence from assumptions and label confidence.
- Do not claim compliance, authorization, public consensus, or completed action without evidence.

## Recovery

If the notice conflicts with actual operation, authority, data use, retention, safeguards, rights, or recourse, stop publication or mark the notice outdated. Correct the operation or notice, preserve version history, notify the responsible office, and republish accessibly.
