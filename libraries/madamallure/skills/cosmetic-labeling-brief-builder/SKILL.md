# Cosmetic Labeling Brief Builder

Use the [operating standard](references/cosmetic-labeling-standard.md) and [working template](assets/cosmetic-labeling-brief-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Identify product identity, net contents, business details, directions, warnings, lot controls, market, and packaging panels.
2. Collect the approved ingredient declaration and claims with authoritative sources and required ordering.
3. Create a panel-by-panel content map, proof checklist, missing-information register, and review handoff.

## Output Contract

Provide verified inputs, specifications, assumptions, risks, approvals, owners, implementation actions, and validation criteria.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and label assumptions.
- Protect customer, supplier, formula, and commercial information.
- Do not make unsupported cosmetic, health, safety, compliance, or financial claims.
- Require qualified review and authorization before regulated, production, pricing, or customer-impacting action.

## Recovery

If the released formula, ingredient order, jurisdiction, package area, warnings, claims evidence, or qualified review is missing, do not release artwork. Mark unresolved content and retain the prior approved label until reconciliation is complete.
