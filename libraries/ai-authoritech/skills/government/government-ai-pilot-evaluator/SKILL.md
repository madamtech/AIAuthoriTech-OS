# Government AI Pilot Evaluator

Use the [pilot evaluation standard](references/pilot-evaluation-standard.md) and the [pilot evaluation template](assets/pilot-evaluation-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Confirm approved hypothesis, baseline, population, duration, metrics, risk controls, oversight, stop rules, and evidence plan.
2. Measure outcomes and harms, data quality, human workload, failure modes, user feedback, subgroup effects, incidents, costs, and vendor performance.
3. Recommend scale, extend, redesign, limit, stop, or research with conditions and monitoring.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, authority, evidence, assumptions, findings, risks, controls, owners, decisions, and validation or monitoring requirements.

## Guardrails

- Verify current jurisdiction, policy, legal, records, procurement, privacy, security, and accessibility requirements with qualified officials.
- Protect sensitive, personal, security, vendor, and deliberative information.
- Separate observed evidence from assumptions and label confidence.
- Do not claim compliance, authorization, public consensus, or completed action without evidence.

## Recovery

If approved scope, baseline, population, controls, data quality, independence, or stop-rule evidence is compromised, suspend the verdict and pilot expansion. Protect participants, preserve evidence, and require corrected evaluation or pilot redesign.
