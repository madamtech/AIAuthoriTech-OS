# Ontology Standard

Begin with approved competency questions and the inferences that must or must not
be supported. Reuse governed namespaces where semantics and licenses fit. Define
stable IRIs, classes, properties, domains, ranges, cardinalities, constraints,
provenance, temporal behavior, identity, uncertainty, and open- or closed-world
assumptions. Separate universal domain semantics from organization policy and
observed facts. Validate syntax, logical consistency, satisfiability, prohibited
inferences, example data, competency questions, mappings, round trips, consumer
behavior, version migration, and continued resolution of deprecated identifiers.
