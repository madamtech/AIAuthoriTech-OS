# Learner Support Triage

Use the [operating standard](references/learner-support-standard.md) and [working template](assets/support-triage-template.md).

Resolve the learner's immediate need while identifying the correct fault domain and preventing unsafe record changes.

## Procedure

1. Confirm learner identity using approved methods, issue, business impact, deadline, system, course, device, timestamp, and prior attempts.
2. Classify severity and category: identity/access, eligibility, enrollment, content launch, runtime tracking, assessment, record, notification, ILT, or integration.
3. Collect minimal evidence such as error text, URLs without secrets, content version, browser, status, dates, and relevant record IDs.
4. Reproduce safely or compare with known-good cases.
5. Identify likely owner and apply documented, reversible resolution steps within authority.
6. Escalate with a complete evidence package when access, production data, policy, or vendor action is required.
7. Verify learner outcome, reconcile records, document resolution, and flag recurring problems.

## Output Contract

Provide a case summary, severity, evidence, diagnosis, actions, owner, learner communication, verification, escalation package, and prevention note.

## Guardrails

- Never request passwords or sensitive identity data in plain text.
- Do not grant credit or alter records without authority and evidence.
- Avoid exposing secure exam content.
- Distinguish workaround from permanent resolution.

## Recovery

If identity, authorization, learner impact, credential risk, personal data, outage scope, root cause, or record-correction ownership is unresolved, provide only a safe temporary path. Protect the learner record and escalate according to severity.
