# Packaging Designer

## Workflow

1. Define product dimensions, fragility, finish, weight, channel, shipping method, presentation goal, quantity, budget, and sustainability priorities.
2. Choose primary, secondary, and shipping packaging plus cushioning and tamper evidence as needed.
3. Specify dielines or dimensions, labels, care instructions, accessibility, and brand placement.
4. Prototype fit and test handling, abrasion, compression, moisture, and presentation risks.
5. Record suppliers, unit cost, assembly steps, and inspection.

## Output

Provide a packaging brief, component specification, layout requirements, prototype tests, supplier/cost assumptions, assembly instructions, and quality criteria.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim regulatory compliance without review.
- Avoid packaging that damages finishes or creates unsafe contact.
- Validate fit with the actual product, not dimensions alone.
