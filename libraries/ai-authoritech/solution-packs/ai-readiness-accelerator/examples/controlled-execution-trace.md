# Controlled Execution Trace

| Stage | Inputs checked | Decision | Output | Recovery or gate exercised |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intake | Sponsor, objectives, systems, exclusions | Proceed with synthetic evidence | Engagement control record | Autonomous pricing and direct changes excluded |
| Discovery | Proposal log, interviews, rework sample, systems | Treat interview and rework claims as medium confidence | Current-state findings | Contradictions remain visible |
| Analysis | Readiness, opportunities, governance, risk, ROI | Block broad deployment | Ranked portfolio and guarded ROI scenario | Missing governance and security controls override value score |
| Strategy | Owners, dependencies, gates, measures | Recommend contained pilot | 30/60/90-day roadmap | Data remediation precedes generation |
| Client validation | Synthetic sponsor decisions | Accept pilot conditions | Decision log | No implementation authority inferred |
| Commercialization | Roadmap scope | Keep proposal and SOW hypothetical | Commercial handoff requirements | No price or contract represented as approved |
| Quality gate | Evidence, arithmetic, scope, risks | Conditional pass | Controlled-test record | False precision and unsupported scaling claims rejected |

## Behavioral observations

- The workflow applied mandatory governance and security gates before discretionary ROI ranking.
- A wide value range did not become a guaranteed savings claim.
- The roadmap, hypothetical commercial scope, and implementation boundaries remained aligned.
- No sensitive production data or external action was required.

## Remaining evidence gap

A real, authorized engagement is still required to evaluate interview quality, evidence collection burden, stakeholder disagreement, deliverable usability, pricing acceptance, and sustained outcomes. This controlled test does not justify maturity promotion by itself.
