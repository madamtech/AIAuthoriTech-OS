# Cosmetic Formulation Safety Standard

Use current primary sources and retain source dates in every formula record:

- FDA MoCRA and safety substantiation: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/modernization-cosmetics-regulation-act-2022-mocra
- FDA homemade cosmetics: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/resources-industry-cosmetics/small-businesses-homemade-cosmetics-fact-sheet
- FDA labeling: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-labeling
- FDA aromatherapy claims: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/aromatherapy
- IFRA Standards: https://ifrafragrance.org/initiatives-positions/safe-use-fragrance-science/ifra-standards

Use supplier specifications, safety data, certificates, and current intended-use
limits. Maintain safety-substantiation records. Treat custom formulas as new
products requiring their own compatibility, stability, microbial, and labeling
review when composition or intended use materially changes.
