# Proposal evidence standard

For every material claim record:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| claim | Exact proposal assertion |
| type | fact, estimate, assumption, recommendation, credential, or commitment |
| source | Approved artifact or accountable owner |
| date | Evidence date |
| confidence | high, medium, low, or unknown |
| approval | Required approver and status |
| treatment | state, qualify, omit, or resolve |

## Required checks

- Outcomes trace to discovery or approved strategy.
- Quantified value traces to an approved business case.
- Dates trace to capacity and dependency evidence.
- Fees trace to an approved pricing decision.
- Credentials and examples are verifiable and permitted for use.
- No proposal language accidentally creates a guarantee or binding acceptance term.
