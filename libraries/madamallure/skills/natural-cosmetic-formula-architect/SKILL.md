# Natural Cosmetic Formula Architect

## Procedure

1. Define product type, use site, user, format, performance, sensory target,
   packaging, batch size, shelf-life target, and market.
2. Classify the system as anhydrous, emulsion, aqueous, surfactant, alkaline soap,
   effervescent, fragrance oil, or hybrid. Route to the specialist skill when one
   applies.
3. Build a 100-percent formula with ingredient identity, phase, function, use rate,
   supplier, lot, specification, and source for every limit.
4. Preserve MadamAllure's alcohol-free and natural-oil-and-herb preference. Clearly
   identify DPG, IPM, preservatives, solubilizers, colorants, or other materials
   that do not meet a selected natural standard; never relabel them as natural.
5. Check intended-use restrictions, supplier documentation, allergens, sensitizers,
   oxidation, photosensitivity, color-additive status, packaging compatibility,
   and current IFRA limits for fragrance materials.
6. For water-containing or water-exposed products, require a justified broad-
   spectrum preservation system and microbial testing plan.
7. Define manufacturing order, temperatures, mixing, hold points, in-process
   checks, yield, fill, quarantine, and batch-record requirements.
8. Define stability, compatibility, safety-substantiation, and consumer-use tests.
9. Separate cosmetic appearance or cleansing claims from drug claims.
10. Deliver with [assets/formula-development-record.md](assets/formula-development-record.md)
    and apply [references/cosmetic-formulation-safety-standard.md](references/cosmetic-formulation-safety-standard.md).

## Output Contract

Return a formula brief totaling 100 percent, gram scale for the requested batch,
ingredient and claim rationale, process, test plan, labeling handoff, unresolved
supplier data, assumptions, risks, and a status of concept, bench trial, testing,
approved master formula, or production hold.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim that natural means safe, nonallergenic, preservative-free, or legal.
- Do not invent usage limits, IFRA compliance, INCI names, shelf life, pH, or claims.
- Do not release a formula for sale from calculations alone.
- Do not recommend water-based products without preservation and microbial controls.
- Do not convert therapeutic aromatherapy claims into cosmetic claims.
- Require qualified review for toxicology, microbiology, regulatory, and medical issues.

## Recovery

If supplier limits, identity, safety data, or intended use are missing, place the
affected ingredient or formula on hold and list the evidence required. If the
formula does not total 100 percent, contains incompatible phases, or lacks a safe
manufacturing path, return it for reformulation rather than filling gaps silently.
