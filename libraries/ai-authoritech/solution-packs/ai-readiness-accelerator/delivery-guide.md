# AI Readiness Accelerator Delivery Guide

## Engagement gates

1. Confirm signed authority, confidentiality, scope, stakeholders, evidence access, dates, and exclusions.
2. Complete discovery without treating missing evidence as a zero or an unsupported fact.
3. Reconcile contradictions and obtain stakeholder validation before prioritizing opportunities.
4. Apply governance, feasibility, risk, and value gates before recommending a pilot.
5. Obtain sponsor decisions before converting the roadmap into commercial scope.
6. Independently verify traceability, calculations, scope consistency, and acceptance criteria before delivery.

## Standard delivery sequence

Use `AA-WFL-000001`. Store a stable engagement ID on every artifact and maintain one evidence inventory, assumption register, decision log, and risk register throughout the engagement.

## Packaging rule

The readiness report and roadmap are consulting findings. A proposal or statement of work is a separate commercial offer and must not imply approval, procurement, deployment, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Field-testing rule

For each live or controlled engagement, record the scenario, sanitized inputs, expected behavior, observed behavior, failures, corrections, reviewer, approval, and reusable lesson. Do not store client-confidential material in the public library.
