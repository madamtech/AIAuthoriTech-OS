# Anhydrous Formulation Standard

Treat the formula as a complete finished product. Confirm supplier use limits,
fragrance restrictions, allergen information, oxidation sensitivity, botanical
identity, packaging compatibility, and foreseeable water exposure. Use weight
percent, not volume or drops, for reproducible formulas.

Record butter and wax melt history because cooling and agitation affect crystal
structure and texture. Validate antioxidant choice separately from microbial risk.
Require bench, stability, and packaging trials before approving a master formula.
