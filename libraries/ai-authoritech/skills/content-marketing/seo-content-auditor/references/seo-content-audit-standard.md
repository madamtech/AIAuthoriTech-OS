# SEO Content Audit Standard

Audit a dated, crawlable content set using verified URLs, status, canonicals, indexability, metadata, headings, internal links, structured data, content purpose, search intent, originality, accessibility, performance context, conversions, and authoritative analytics where available. Distinguish technical, content, authority, and measurement issues. Avoid guarantees, keyword stuffing, fabricated volume, or destructive pruning without traffic, backlink, conversion, business, and redirect evidence.
