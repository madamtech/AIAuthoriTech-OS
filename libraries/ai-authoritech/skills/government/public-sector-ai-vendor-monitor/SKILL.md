# Public Sector AI Vendor Monitor

Use the [vendor monitoring standard](references/vendor-monitoring-standard.md) and the [vendor scorecard template](assets/vendor-scorecard-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Translate contract requirements into measurable evidence, owners, thresholds, cadence, and escalation paths.
2. Review service levels, incidents, changes, model/data updates, subcontractors, audit artifacts, defects, complaints, and corrective actions.
3. Assess impact, require remediation, verify closure, track concentration risk, and maintain transition and data-return readiness.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, authority, evidence, assumptions, findings, risks, controls, owners, decisions, and validation or monitoring requirements.

## Guardrails

- Verify current jurisdiction, policy, legal, records, procurement, privacy, security, and accessibility requirements with qualified officials.
- Protect sensitive, personal, security, vendor, and deliberative information.
- Separate observed evidence from assumptions and label confidence.
- Do not claim compliance, authorization, public consensus, or completed action without evidence.

## Recovery

If a material breach, incident, unapproved change, evidence gap, or exit-readiness failure occurs, preserve contract evidence and escalate through authorized procurement and program channels. Apply only approved containment, remedies, or transition actions.
