# Executive Proposal Builder

Make the decision, value, scope, and obligations easy to evaluate.

## Procedure

1. Confirm proposal owner, recipient, decision requested, validity period, brand
   requirements, approved commercial inputs, and source evidence.
2. Build a claim ledger using
   [references/proposal-evidence-standard.md](references/proposal-evidence-standard.md).
3. State the client's situation, desired outcomes, and material constraints without
   exaggerating urgency or certainty.
4. Present the recommended approach as phases with outcome, included work,
   deliverables, validation, dependencies, and decision gates.
5. Separate included scope, optional scope, exclusions, assumptions, client
   responsibilities, and third-party dependencies.
6. Present delivery options only when they represent meaningful tradeoffs.
7. Use approved ROI ranges and confidence labels; never upgrade hypotheses into
   promised results.
8. Include governance, security, privacy, change, adoption, and measurement work
   required for the proposed outcome.
9. State fees, payment structure, expenses, taxes, dates, and terms only from
   approved inputs. Mark missing commercial decisions clearly.
10. Deliver with [assets/executive-proposal-template.md](assets/executive-proposal-template.md).

## Guardrails

- Do not invent testimonials, credentials, case studies, partners, certifications,
  legal terms, client facts, or pricing.
- Do not imply guaranteed savings, compliance, adoption, accuracy, or delivery dates.
- Do not copy confidential findings into a proposal beyond the approved audience.
- Keep the proposal persuasive through relevance and evidence, not unsupported claims.
- Route binding scope, acceptance, legal terms, and change control to a Statement of
  Work and qualified review.
- Require explicit approval before sending, publishing, or representing the proposal
  as accepted.

## Output Contract

Provide the proposal, claim ledger, unresolved inputs, approval checklist, and
items requiring SOW drafting, legal review, financial approval, security review,
or implementation planning.

## Recovery

If approved evidence or commercial inputs are missing, produce a clearly marked
draft with an unresolved-input register instead of inventing them. If sources
conflict, retain the conflict and identify the accountable approver. Never send,
publish, or represent the draft as approved without explicit authorization.
