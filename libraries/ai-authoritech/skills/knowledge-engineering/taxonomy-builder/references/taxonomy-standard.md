# Taxonomy Standard

Give every concept a stable nonsemantic identifier, preferred label, definition,
scope note, synonyms, examples, exclusions, status, owner, and version. Use broader,
narrower, related, and faceted relationships deliberately; reject cycles and
ambiguous parentage unless documented polyhierarchy is required. Derive concepts
from representative corpus evidence and user language. Test classification
agreement, findability, coverage, balance, orphans, unknown handling, sensitive
attributes, and real navigation or reporting tasks. Preserve deprecated IDs and
publish mappings for merges, splits, moves, replacements, and external vocabularies.
