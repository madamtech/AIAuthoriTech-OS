# Document Intelligence Standard

Inventory every document by stable ID, exact version, date, owner, authority,
format, page count, attachments, access class, extraction method, and limitations.
Preserve logical and visual structure, including headings, tables, footnotes,
signatures, annotations, and cross-references. Record every finding as source
statement, normalized observation, or analyst inference with exact location and
confidence. Compare documents only after aligning versions and authority. Keep
additions, deletions, conflicts, missing material, OCR uncertainty, and inaccessible
content visible. Validate high-risk and low-confidence findings against originals
and report the reviewed denominator rather than implying full coverage.
