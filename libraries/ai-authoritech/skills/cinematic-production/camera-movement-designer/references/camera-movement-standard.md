# Camera Movement Standard

Choose movement only when it supports story perspective, emotion, reveal, geography, rhythm, or transition. Define start and end frames, axis, path, speed, acceleration, subject blocking, lens, focus, stabilization, equipment, operator needs, floor and overhead clearance, rehearsal, edit handles, and safety. Prefer the simplest effective method and require qualified planning for cranes, drones, vehicles, cable systems, elevated rigs, crowds, or hazardous terrain.
