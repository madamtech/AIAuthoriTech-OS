# Cosmetic Labeling Standard

Build the brief from the verified formula, ingredient nomenclature and order, net contents, business identity, contact information, batch traceability, directions, warnings, allergens, claims, package dimensions, legibility, language, jurisdiction, and channel requirements. Never convert marketing intent into an unsubstantiated drug claim. Treat the brief as preparation for qualified legal and regulatory review, and reconcile it against the released formula and artwork before printing.
