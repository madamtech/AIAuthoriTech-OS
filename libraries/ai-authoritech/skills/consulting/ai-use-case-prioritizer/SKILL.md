# AI Use Case Prioritizer

Prioritize comparable use cases, not persuasive pitch quality.

1. Normalize every submission into problem, user, decision or task, current
   baseline, proposed AI role, data, expected outcome, owner, and evidence.
2. Merge duplicates and split proposals containing independently valuable jobs.
3. Screen for genuine AI fit. Route deterministic integration or rules problems
   to conventional automation analysis.
4. Score supported criteria with
   [references/use-case-scoring.md](references/use-case-scoring.md).
5. Mark unsupported criteria `insufficient evidence`; never award a neutral score.
6. Apply prohibited-use, safety, privacy, security, legal, control, and ownership
   gates independently of the numerical result.
7. Adjust the portfolio for dependencies, organizational capacity, concentration
   risk, shared enablers, learning value, and outcome diversity.
8. Assign one decision:
   - discover further;
   - experiment;
   - prepare for implementation;
   - scale;
   - defer pending prerequisites;
   - reject.
9. Define the smallest evidence-generating next step and a measurable exit criterion.
10. Deliver with [assets/use-case-portfolio-template.md](assets/use-case-portfolio-template.md).

## Rules

- Trace claimed value to a baseline and named beneficiary.
- Penalize unclear AI roles, not unfamiliar technology.
- Do not rank unsafe or prohibited cases into acceptability.
- Keep estimated benefits as ranges with confidence.
- Do not let one department consume the portfolio solely because it submitted more
  ideas.
- Re-score when material evidence, controls, cost, or capacity changes.

## Handoff

Provide ranked and gated portfolios, score evidence, shared prerequisites,
recommended next tests, exit criteria, assumptions, and cases requiring automation
analysis, governance review, ROI modeling, architecture, or roadmap updates.
