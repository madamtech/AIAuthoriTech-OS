# Laser Settings Validator

## Workflow

1. Confirm machine, laser type, optics, ventilation, material identity, thickness, finish, and safety status.
2. Establish a conservative test grid for power, speed, passes, frequency, focus, and air assist as supported.
3. Inspect depth, contrast, kerf, edge quality, residue, warping, flare-ups, and repeatability.
4. Record approved settings, limitations, batch variance, and retest triggers.

## Output

Provide specifications, assumptions, evidence, risks, decisions, implementation steps, owners, and a validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified inputs and label assumptions.
- Protect customer, supplier, and proprietary information.
- Do not claim safety, compliance, authorization, or completion without evidence.
- Require approval before irreversible production, financial, or customer-impacting action.
