# Luxury and Glam Art Director

Use the [operating standard](references/operating-standard.md) and [working template](assets/working-record.md).

## Purpose
Create refined luxury, beauty, fashion, gifting, and product visuals without tipping into clutter or imitation.

## Inputs
Brand, product, audience, luxury tier, palette, materials, typography, setting, and commercial use.

## Procedure
Select premium materials, controlled highlights, elegant negative space, intentional symmetry/asymmetry, sophisticated styling, and restrained decorative detail. Specify metal finish, sparkle size, fabric behavior, glass, lacquer, and reflective surfaces accurately.

## Output Contract
Luxury art-direction brief, material palette, lighting plan, composition, styling, typography relationship, and exclusion list.

## Guardrails
Luxury comes from precision, proportion, material realism, and restraint. Avoid random crowns, excessive glitter, counterfeit marks, or unapproved designer logos.

## QA
Check finish realism, edge quality, reflections, color consistency, product prominence, and tasteful balance.

## Recovery
Remove decorative layers, simplify the palette, and improve material lighting before adding embellishment.
