# Emulsion Safety Standard

Treat every water-containing cosmetic as a microbiologically vulnerable system.
Document water quality, sanitation, pH, preservative supplier guidance, packaging,
use conditions, stability, microbial limits, and preservative-efficacy evidence.
Confirm ingredient identity and current intended-use limits before bench work.

Maintain dated records supporting cosmetic safety and route therapeutic claims,
high-risk populations, and failed microbial results to qualified specialists.
