# Case Study Builder

Use the [operating standard](references/case-study-standard.md) and [working template](assets/case-study-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Confirm permission, audience, objective, confidentiality, source evidence, participants, metrics, timeline, and review process.
2. Develop situation, stakes, constraints, approach, implementation, outcomes, lessons, and next steps with traceable support.
3. Verify quotations and calculations, label attribution or estimates, obtain customer approval, and create channel variants.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, verified inputs, analysis, recommendations or deliverables, assumptions, owners, approvals, measurement, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and protect confidential client information.
- Do not invent performance, customers, quotations, research, rankings, or claims.
- Respect copyright, trademarks, privacy, permissions, and platform rules.
- Separate observation from inference and label assumptions.

## Recovery

If permission, source records, attribution, metric definitions, confidentiality, quotation accuracy, or approval is unresolved, anonymize or remove the affected claim and keep the case study unpublished.
