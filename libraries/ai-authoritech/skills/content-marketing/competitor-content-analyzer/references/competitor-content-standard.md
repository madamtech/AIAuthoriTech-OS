# Competitor Content Analysis Standard

Analyze lawfully accessible, dated public content with recorded URLs, capture dates, market scope, comparison criteria, audience, topics, formats, channels, frequency, positioning, proof, engagement signals, and limitations. Separate observed facts from inference, normalize unequal samples, and avoid scraping restrictions, confidential information, trademark misuse, plagiarism, or claims about internal strategy. Use findings for differentiated decisions, not copying, and preserve citations.
