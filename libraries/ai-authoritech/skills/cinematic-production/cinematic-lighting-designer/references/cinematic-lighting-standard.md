# Cinematic Lighting Standard

Design lighting from story mood, time, location, blocking, skin tones, production design, camera and lens, exposure, contrast, color, continuity, power, rigging, heat, weather, schedule, equipment, crew, and safety. Define motivation, key, fill, backlight, practicals, negative fill, control, color temperature, ratios, cues, and measurement. Require qualified electrical and rigging review and never overload circuits, defeat protections, or expose people to unsafe heat, glare, height, or weather.
