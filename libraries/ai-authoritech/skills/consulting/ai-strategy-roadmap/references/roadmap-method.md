# Roadmap method

## Horizons

Use horizons instead of false precision when delivery evidence is incomplete:

1. **Now — align and enable:** confirm ownership, policy, data, controls, baseline,
   architecture constraints, and portfolio decisions.
2. **Next — prove value:** run bounded experiments with success measures, oversight,
   time limits, and explicit scale/stop criteria.
3. **Later — implement and scale:** productionize proven cases, integrate operating
   controls, train users, monitor outcomes, and retire ineffective work.

Map horizons to calendar dates only when capacity, dependencies, procurement, and
decision timelines are supported.

## Initiative gate

Every initiative must answer:

- Which business outcome changes?
- What baseline supports the need?
- Which prerequisite capabilities are required?
- Who owns the outcome and risk?
- What is the smallest testable scope?
- Which measure proves or disproves value?
- Which control evidence permits progression?
- What triggers scale, redesign, pause, or termination?

## Portfolio balance

Do not optimize solely for total opportunity score. Consider dependencies, risk
reduction, learning value, organizational capacity, concentration risk, and the
ability to measure actual outcomes.
