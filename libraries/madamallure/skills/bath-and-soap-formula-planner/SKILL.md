# Bath and Soap Formula Planner

## Procedure

1. Classify the product as effervescent, melt, salt, syndet, melt-and-pour, or lye soap.
2. Define intended use, tub or shower conditions, slip risk, packaging, humidity, batch size, and claims.
3. Build the 100-percent formula and verify every supplier limit and fragrance use category.
4. For bath bombs and truffles, control acid-base balance, liquid binder, humidity, expansion, hardness, residue, color migration, and tub slip.
5. For lye soap, require a current trusted calculator, verified oil SAP data, alkali purity,
   superfat policy, water calculation, independent recalculation, PPE, ventilation, and cure plan.
6. Never merge bath-bomb and soap calculation methods.
7. Define bench trials, pH or free-alkali checks where applicable, stability, use testing,
   packaging, and batch records using [assets/bath-soap-formula-record.md](assets/bath-soap-formula-record.md).
8. Apply [references/bath-soap-safety-standard.md](references/bath-soap-safety-standard.md).

## Output Contract

Return product classification, 100-percent formula, batch weights, calculation
evidence, process and safety controls, test plan, packaging, claims handoff, and status.

## Guardrails

- Do not calculate lye from memory or generic ratios.
- Do not recommend therapeutic, detoxifying, pain-relief, or disease claims.
- Do not assume natural colorants or essential oils are unrestricted.
- Do not release caustic or failed batches for sale or skin use.

## Recovery

If SAP values, alkali purity, fragrance limits, or product classification are uncertain,
stop calculation. Quarantine batches with unexpected heat, expansion, free alkali,
irritation, contamination, or unstable color and route them to qualified review.
