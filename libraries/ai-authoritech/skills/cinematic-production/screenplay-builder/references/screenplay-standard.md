# Screenplay Standard

Translate an approved story into a production-readable screenplay with scene headings, locations, time, action, characters, dialogue, transitions only when needed, pacing, runtime, continuity, props, content boundaries, and revision control. Show rather than explain, keep action filmable, and maintain character voice and causal sequence. Protect rights, avoid unapproved likenesses, and flag stunts, minors, intimacy, weapons, or other specialist-review needs.
