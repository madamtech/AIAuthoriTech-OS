# Gift Box Designer

Use the [operating standard](references/gift-box-design-standard.md) and [working template](assets/gift-box-design-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define recipient, occasion, theme, budget, products, personalization, quantity, delivery, and presentation goal.
2. Select compatible contents and specify box, insert, cushioning, labels, message, reveal order, and shipping protection.
3. Prototype fit, calculate unit cost and labor, document assembly, and set inspection criteria.

## Output Contract

Provide verified inputs, specifications, assumptions, risks, approvals, owners, implementation actions, and validation criteria.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and label assumptions.
- Protect customer, supplier, formula, and commercial information.
- Do not make unsupported cosmetic, health, safety, compliance, or financial claims.
- Require qualified review and authorization before regulated, production, pricing, or customer-impacting action.

## Recovery

If product compatibility, allergen communication, dimensions, stock, shipping protection, personalization proof, cost, or approval is unresolved, keep the box in draft. Do not promise a delivery date or substitute products without authorization.
