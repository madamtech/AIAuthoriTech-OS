# Website Messaging Audit Standard

Audit against a captured page version, audience, business objective, offer, value proposition, evidence, navigation context, conversion path, brand voice, accessibility, and device constraints. Separate observation from inference and prioritize clarity, relevance, differentiation, proof, objections, action, consistency, and comprehension. Verify quotations and screenshots, avoid fabricated user behavior, and rank recommendations by impact, confidence, effort, dependency, and owner.
