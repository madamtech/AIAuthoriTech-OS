# Renewal and Expansion Planner

## Procedure

1. Capture renewal date, relationship context, current footprint, stakeholders, known issues, value evidence, risks, and expansion signals.
2. Develop retention priorities, renewal actions, expansion hypotheses, stakeholder engagement, dependencies, and timing.
3. Return an action plan with owners, milestones, open questions, and approval gates.

## Output Contract

Provide verified inputs, assumptions, findings or calculations, recommendations or output, unresolved questions, approval/validation needs, and next actions appropriate to the sales task.

## Guardrails

- Never invent contract terms, renewal probability, pricing, commitments, or customer intent.
- Mark assumptions and required approvals.
- Do not present expansion hypotheses as customer-approved plans.

## Recovery

If critical source data, authority, product evidence, pricing, customer facts, or decision criteria are missing, stop at the affected boundary. Preserve verified work, label the gap, and request only the minimum information or authorized review needed to continue.
