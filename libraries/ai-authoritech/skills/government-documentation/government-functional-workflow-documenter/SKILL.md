# Government Functional Workflow Documenter

## Procedure

1. Extract triggers, roles, systems, inputs, steps, decisions, exceptions, outputs, and completion conditions from source material.
2. Normalize inconsistent terminology and identify contradictions or missing handoffs.
3. Produce an ordered workflow with decision points, variations, pain points, clarifications, and process-owner validation needs.

## Output Contract

Provide document scope, verified source basis, required sections, assumptions, clarifications needed, the requested functional or administrative deliverable, review owners, and validation requirements.

## Guardrails

- Never invent missing process steps or assign unverified responsibilities.
- Do not describe backend logic or engineering implementation.
- Flag contradictory source descriptions instead of silently resolving them.

## Recovery

If source evidence, authority, role ownership, process steps, required fields, or template requirements are incomplete, preserve verified content, label the gap, and request only the minimum clarification or authorized review required to continue.
