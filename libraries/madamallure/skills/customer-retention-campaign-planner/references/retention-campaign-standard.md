# Customer Retention Campaign Standard

Plan retention activity from a defined business objective, eligible audience, lawful contact basis, consent and suppression status, purchase behavior, lifecycle stage, channel, frequency, offer economics, inventory, capacity, message, claims, personalization, accessibility, timing, attribution, stop rules, and owner approval. Protect customer data, avoid manipulative urgency or sensitive inference, test with a controlled segment, and measure incrementality rather than vanity metrics alone.
