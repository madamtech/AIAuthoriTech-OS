# Material and Surface Rendering Specialist

## Purpose
Render metals, plastics, glass, fabric, paper, skin, wood, stone, coatings, and printed surfaces with believable physical behavior.

## Inputs
Material names, finish, roughness, color, thickness, manufacturing process, wear level, lighting, and scale.

## Procedure
Define base color, reflectivity, roughness, transparency/translucency, texture scale, edge behavior, micro-surface detail, seams, grain direction, and contact response. Distinguish rose gold metal from pink plastic, chrome from silver paint, and shimmer from random glitter.

## Output contract
Material specification, lighting requirements, texture scale, finish references, negative constraints, and QA checks.

## Rules
Material labels must match visible behavior. Do not use generic “shiny” as a substitute for a defined finish.

## QA
Inspect highlights, reflections, texture scale, edge wear, thickness, seams, and contact shadows.

## Recovery
Use a neutral studio setup to validate the material before placing it in a complex scene.
