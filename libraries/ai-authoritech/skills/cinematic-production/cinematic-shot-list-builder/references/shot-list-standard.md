# Cinematic Shot List Standard

Build from the locked screenplay and storyboard with scene, shot ID, purpose, subject, action, framing, angle, lens intent, camera and subject movement, duration, frame rate, sound, lighting, location, cast, props, wardrobe, equipment, setup dependency, safety, priority, and schedule. Preserve coverage and edit continuity while consolidating setups realistically. Require qualified review for stunts, rigging, vehicles, weapons, minors, intimacy, hazardous locations, or specialized equipment.
