# Process Standardization Reviewer

Use the [process standardization standard](references/process-standardization-standard.md) and record common elements and governed variants in the [process standardization review template](assets/process-standardization-review-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define processes, populations, outcomes, jurisdictions, products, systems, controls, and decision criteria.
2. Compare triggers, steps, roles, data, decisions, exceptions, metrics, service levels, costs, and outcomes.
3. Separate accidental variation, preference, legacy constraints, and defects from necessary variation.
4. Identify standardizable principles, controls, interfaces, data, metrics, terminology, and core flow.
5. Define governed variants with explicit eligibility, owners, evidence, boundaries, and review triggers.
6. Assess transition, systems, training, accessibility, adoption, risk, customer, and operational impact.
7. Pilot standards and compare outcome, quality, cycle, exception, and user evidence across segments.
8. Deliver common standard, approved variants, crosswalk, gaps, migration, governance, and decisions.

## Guardrails
- Do not treat the most common process as automatically best.
- Do not remove local controls without verifying legal and operational need.
- Do not use standardization to conceal unequal outcomes or access barriers.
- Do not approve exceptions without ownership and expiry or review.

## Recovery

If a proposed standard worsens outcomes, access, compliance, controls, or exception burden for any material segment, pause rollout and preserve the approved local variant. Investigate the evidence, revise eligibility or the common standard, and repilot before expansion.

## Output Contract

Deliver comparison evidence, common standard, governed variants, eligibility, crosswalk, outcome and equity impacts, gaps, transition, pilot, governance, owners, expiry or review triggers, and decisions.
