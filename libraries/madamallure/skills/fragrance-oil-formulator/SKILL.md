# Fragrance Oil Formulator

## Procedure

1. Define product type, use site, package, audience, scent direction, batch size,
   oxidation target, and natural-positioning requirement.
2. Classify every material as fragrance concentrate, essential oil, carrier oil,
   antioxidant, DPG, IPM, or other functional ingredient. State plainly that DPG
   and IPM are optional functional materials and may not meet a chosen natural standard.
3. Build the concentrate and finished-product formula by weight, totaling 100 percent.
4. Determine the correct IFRA finished-product category and obtain current supplier
   IFRA documentation. Use the lowest applicable finished-product limit; do not
   confuse concentrate percentage with an individual restricted-material limit.
5. Review sensitization, photosensitivity, oxidation, allergens, dermal use,
   packaging compatibility, staining, viscosity, roller performance, and scent stability.
6. Define blending, maturation, filtration, fill, retention sample, and batch controls.
7. Deliver with [assets/fragrance-oil-formula-record.md](assets/fragrance-oil-formula-record.md)
   and apply [references/fragrance-oil-safety-standard.md](references/fragrance-oil-safety-standard.md).

## Output Contract

Return scent brief, concentrate and finished formula, batch weights, carrier and
diluent rationale, IFRA evidence, process, stability and compatibility tests,
labeling handoff, natural-positioning disclosure, risks, and status.

## Guardrails

- Do not convert drops or scent preference into a safety limit.
- Do not assume essential oils are safer because they are natural.
- Do not call DPG or IPM natural without a defined standard and supporting evidence.
- Do not make anxiety, sleep, pain, hormonal, antimicrobial, or disease claims.
- Do not infer IFRA compliance without the current formula and intended-use category.

## Recovery

If fragrance composition, supplier certificate, category, or limit is unavailable,
place the scented formula on hold. If haze, separation, irritation, oxidation, or
package incompatibility occurs, preserve the sample and run a controlled reformulation.
