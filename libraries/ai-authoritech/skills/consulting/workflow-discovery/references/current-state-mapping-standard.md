# Current-State Mapping Standard

Assign stable IDs to steps, decisions, handoffs, exceptions, and evidence. For
each step capture actor, system, input, action, output, handling time, wait time,
frequency, control, and evidence label.

Map the primary path and material variants. Record what happens in practice, not
only what the SOP prescribes. Keep observations, participant reports, documents,
and inferences distinguishable.

A map is validated only when accountable stakeholders confirm scope, material
variants, controls, and unresolved disagreements. Otherwise mark it provisional.
