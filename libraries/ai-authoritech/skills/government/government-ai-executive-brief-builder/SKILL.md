# Government AI Executive Brief Builder

Use the [executive brief standard](references/executive-brief-standard.md) and the [executive brief template](assets/executive-brief-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Clarify executive audience, decision, deadline, scope, authority, evidence, and political or operational context.
2. Synthesize verified facts into mission need, options, benefits, risks, dependencies, financial/resource implications, and stakeholder impact.
3. Recommend a proportionate decision with conditions, owners, milestones, unresolved questions, and supporting annexes.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, evidence, assumptions, findings, risks, options, recommendations, owners, decisions, and validation or monitoring requirements.

## Guardrails

- Verify current jurisdiction, policy, legal, procurement, records, privacy, security, and accessibility requirements with qualified officials.
- Separate evidence from assumptions and label confidence.
- Protect sensitive, personal, procurement, security, and deliberative information.
- Do not claim compliance, authorization, public consensus, or completed government action without evidence.

## Recovery

If material evidence, authority, cost, risk, affected-population impact, or decision options are unresolved, label the brief not decision-ready. Preserve protected annexes separately and assign evidence owners before reissuing.
