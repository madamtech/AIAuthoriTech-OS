# Cinematic Video Prompt Standard

Compose video prompts from the approved start state, end state, action, performance, environment, camera movement, lens, duration, frame rate, timing, lighting, sound intent, continuity anchors, exclusions, and delivery format. Keep motion physically and narratively coherent, control the number of simultaneous changes, and protect likeness and source rights. Version settings, inspect temporal consistency, anatomy, identity, artifacts, flicker, camera behavior, safety, and edit compatibility.
