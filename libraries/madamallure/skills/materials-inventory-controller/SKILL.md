# Materials Inventory Controller

Use the [operating standard](references/materials-inventory-standard.md) and [working template](assets/materials-inventory-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define item IDs, units, variants, lots, shelf life, storage, suppliers, costs, and minimum levels.
2. Record receipts, reservations, usage, waste, adjustments, returns, and cycle counts with traceability.
3. Reconcile variances, quarantine questionable stock, trigger replenishment, and monitor slow or expiring inventory.

## Output Contract

Provide verified inputs, specifications, assumptions, risks, approvals, execution steps, owners, and validation criteria.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and label estimates.
- Protect customer, supplier, and proprietary information.
- Require approval before irreversible production, pricing, or customer communication.
- Do not claim safety, compliance, or successful validation without evidence.

## Recovery

If identity, units, lot status, storage condition, physical count, expiration, or adjustment authority is unresolved, quarantine the affected quantity. Do not release, consume, or write off material until reconciled.
