# Knowledge Governance Standard

## Required control domains

Assess purpose and authority, accountable ownership, permitted use, access, privacy, licensing, retention, legal hold, deletion, provenance, quality, publication, refresh, incident response, change control, and audit evidence. A written policy is not an implemented control; identify the system, owner, test, evidence, and review cadence for every claimed control.

## Decision rules

- Treat the most authoritative permitted source as controlling and record conflicts.
- Apply least privilege and data minimization across originals, derivatives, indexes, embeddings, caches, exports, and backups.
- Require a named human owner for approval, exceptions, incidents, and risk acceptance.
- Block publication when authority, rights, access, or required review is unresolved.
- Propagate restriction and deletion decisions to downstream consumers within the approved service level.

## Evidence standard

Classify controls as implemented and tested, implemented but untested, planned, missing, or not applicable. Cite policy, configuration, access test, audit event, approval, or incident exercise as evidence. Never infer compliance from intent.
