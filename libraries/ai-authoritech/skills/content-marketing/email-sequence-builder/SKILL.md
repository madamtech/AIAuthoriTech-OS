# Email Sequence Builder

Use the [operating standard](references/email-sequence-standard.md) and [working template](assets/email-sequence-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define objective, audience, consent, trigger, lifecycle stage, source facts, offer, cadence, exclusions, and success metrics.
2. Map each email's job, value, subject, preheader, body, proof, CTA, timing, and exit condition.
3. Validate personalization, links, claims, accessibility, frequency, unsubscribe, handoffs, and test plan.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, verified inputs, deliverables or findings, assumptions, owners, approvals, measurement, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and protect confidential client information.
- Do not invent results, customers, quotations, research, urgency, or claims.
- Respect copyright, privacy, consent, accessibility, and platform rules.
- Separate evidence from inference and label assumptions.

## Recovery

If consent, trigger, suppression, timing, claims, personalization, unsubscribe, or owner approval is unresolved, keep the automation inactive and exclude ambiguous contacts.
