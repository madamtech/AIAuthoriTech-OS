# Governance control model

Review these domains:

1. mandate, principles, and decision rights
2. system inventory and ownership
3. risk classification and approval gates
4. intended use, prohibited use, and change control
5. data governance, privacy, provenance, and retention
6. security, access, resilience, and misuse protection
7. testing, evaluation, validation, and acceptance criteria
8. fairness, accessibility, affected-party impact, and recourse
9. transparency, disclosure, documentation, and traceability
10. human oversight, competency, and escalation
11. third-party and vendor governance
12. production monitoring, drift, incidents, rollback, and retirement
13. workforce training and acceptable use
14. independent review, metrics, exceptions, and continuous improvement

## Control maturity

- 0 absent
- 1 informal or ad hoc
- 2 documented but not consistently operating
- 3 operating with repeatable evidence
- 4 measured, tested, and improved

Report design and operating maturity separately. Mark `insufficient evidence`
instead of assuming a control fails or operates.
