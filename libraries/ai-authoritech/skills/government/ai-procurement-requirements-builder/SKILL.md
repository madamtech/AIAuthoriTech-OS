# AI Procurement Requirements Builder

Use the [AI procurement standard](references/ai-procurement-standard.md) and the [procurement requirements template](assets/procurement-requirements-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define mission need, users, affected people, data, deployment context, risk, acquisition path, and applicable authority.
2. Specify functional, accessibility, security, privacy, data, model, transparency, testing, audit, support, portability, and exit requirements.
3. Create evaluation criteria, evidence requests, contract controls, acceptance tests, monitoring, and remedies.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, evidence, assumptions, findings, risks, options, recommendations, owners, decisions, and validation or monitoring requirements.

## Guardrails

- Verify current jurisdiction, policy, legal, procurement, records, privacy, security, and accessibility requirements with qualified officials.
- Separate evidence from assumptions and label confidence.
- Protect sensitive, personal, procurement, security, and deliberative information.
- Do not claim compliance, authorization, public consensus, or completed government action without evidence.

## Recovery

If acquisition authority, evaluation integrity, protected procurement data, conflicts, or acceptance evidence is uncertain, pause the affected activity. Preserve the procurement record, restrict access, and route resolution to authorized procurement and legal officials.
