# Wholesale Pricing Standard

Calculate wholesale pricing from verified direct material, labor, packaging, overhead, spoilage, payment, marketplace, freight, taxes, minimum order, case pack, lead time, capacity, target margin, retailer margin, and channel restrictions. Distinguish margin from markup and test discounts, samples, returns, damage, late payment, and shipping scenarios. Document assumptions, approval, effective dates, review triggers, and exceptions without exposing confidential costs unnecessarily.
