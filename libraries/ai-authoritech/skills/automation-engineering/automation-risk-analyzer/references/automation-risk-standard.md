# Automation Risk Standard

Define impact and likelihood scales before rating. Build evidence-linked scenarios covering failure, misuse, abuse, authorization, data quality, privacy, security, fraud, partial execution, duplication, outage, vendor dependency, human oversight, operations, and change. Identify causes, affected parties, exposure, detectability, and reversibility.

Distinguish inherent risk, current risk with implemented controls, target risk after planned controls, and residual risk after verified controls. Classify controls as implemented and tested, implemented but untested, planned, missing, or not applicable. Do not average away severe risks or invent numerical precision. Require a human owner and authorized acceptance for residual risk. Block release when a severe risk lacks effective prevention, detection, correction, recovery, or escalation.
