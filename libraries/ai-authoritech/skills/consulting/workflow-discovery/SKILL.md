# Workflow Discovery

## Procedure

1. Set boundaries: trigger, completion, owner, volume, and variants.
2. Gather participant, artifact, system, log, form, and SOP evidence.
3. Record each step's actor, system, input, action, output, handling time, wait time,
   decision, and evidence status.
4. Capture handoffs, approvals, duplicate entry, rework, exceptions, and controls.
5. Preserve conflicting accounts instead of silently selecting one.
6. Separate observed pain points from root-cause hypotheses.
7. Mark the map provisional until accountable stakeholders validate it.
8. Hand off step IDs, frequency, time, errors, systems, data sensitivity, controls,
   candidate outcomes, and validation status for opportunity analysis.

Apply [references/current-state-mapping-standard.md](references/current-state-mapping-standard.md)
and deliver with [assets/workflow-discovery-template.md](assets/workflow-discovery-template.md).

## Output Contract

Return scope, evidence inventory, current-state step table, decision table,
handoff matrix, exception register, timing summary, pain-point and root-cause
register, process map, open questions, validation status, and analysis handoff.

## Guardrails

Evidence labels: observed, reported, documented, inferred, disputed, missing.
Do not present future state as current state, omit exceptions, treat an SOP as
proof of practice, or call a task automatable solely because it is manual.

Do not redesign, select technology, estimate benefits without baselines, or expose
sensitive operational details beyond the authorized audience. Route redesign to
Process Optimizer and scoring to Automation Opportunity Analysis.

## Recovery

When participants disagree, record parallel variants and the evidence needed to
resolve them. When evidence is sparse, return a provisional map, open-question
log, and focused validation plan rather than presenting assumptions as facts.
