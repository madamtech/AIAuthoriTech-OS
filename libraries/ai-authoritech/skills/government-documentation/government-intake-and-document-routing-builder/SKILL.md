# Government Intake and Document Routing Builder

## Procedure

1. Identify the intake purpose, submitter, required information, receiving role, routing decisions, approvals, handoffs, status tracking, and completion point.
2. Create the intake structure and routing logic in plain functional language.
3. Flag missing ownership, approval authority, required fields, and exception handling for validation.

## Output Contract

Provide document scope, verified source basis, required sections, assumptions, clarifications needed, the requested functional or administrative deliverable, review owners, and validation requirements.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent approval authority, retention policy, access rules, or required data.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
- Do not design workflow software or automation implementation under this skill.

## Recovery

If source evidence, authority, role ownership, process steps, required fields, or template requirements are incomplete, preserve verified content, label the gap, and request only the minimum clarification or authorized review required to continue.
