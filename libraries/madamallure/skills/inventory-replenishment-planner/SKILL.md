# Inventory Replenishment Planner

Use the [operating standard](references/inventory-standard.md) and [working template](assets/inventory-plan-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define items, units, suppliers, lead times, minimums, shelf life, current stock, open orders, and service target.
2. Clean demand history and separate launches, seasonality, promotions, stockouts, and one-time orders.
3. Calculate transparent reorder points and scenario ranges using documented assumptions.
4. Prioritize shortages, excess, substitutions, purchase timing, and monitoring triggers.

## Output Contract

Provide the decision-ready brief, assumptions, required specifications, risks, approvals, implementation actions, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified product, customer, cost, inventory, and brand information.
- Label assumptions and missing approvals; never invent claims or availability.
- Protect customer data, licensed content, and confidential business information.
- Do not present regulated, legal, safety, or trademark conclusions as approved without qualified review.

## Recovery

If on-hand accuracy, committed stock, lead time, minimum order, shelf life, demand, safety stock, cash limit, or supplier reliability is unresolved, show scenarios rather than issuing a firm purchase plan. Require count reconciliation and owner approval.
