# AI Readiness Accelerator Quality Gate

- [ ] Engagement authority, scope, exclusions, and confidentiality are recorded.
- [ ] Findings distinguish verified facts, stakeholder statements, assumptions, and recommendations.
- [ ] Every material finding links to evidence and confidence.
- [ ] Readiness, workflow, opportunity, governance, risk, and ROI results use consistent inputs.
- [ ] Calculations have units, periods, baselines, scenarios, and uncertainty.
- [ ] Prioritized initiatives include owners, dependencies, gates, measures, and stop conditions.
- [ ] Roadmap, proposal, statement of work, and implementation plan use the same approved scope.
- [ ] Client decisions, unresolved questions, limitations, and required approvals are explicit.
- [ ] No external action, implementation, approval, or guaranteed outcome is falsely claimed.
- [ ] Sensitive client evidence is stored only in an authorized client workspace.
