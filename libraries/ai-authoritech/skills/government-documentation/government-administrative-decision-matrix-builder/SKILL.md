# Government Administrative Decision Matrix Builder

## Procedure

1. Collect the decision to be made, authorized options, criteria, weights if supplied, constraints, evidence, risks, and decision owner.
2. Build a transparent matrix and document scoring rationale, unknowns, sensitivity, and tradeoffs.
3. Return findings as decision support with unresolved questions and required approvals.

## Output Contract

Provide document scope, verified source basis, required sections, assumptions, clarifications needed, the requested functional or administrative deliverable, review owners, and validation requirements.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent criteria weights, evidence, authority, or preferred outcomes.
- Do not present a calculated score as an official agency decision.
- Flag legal, policy, procurement, HR, or technical decisions that require qualified review.

## Recovery

If source evidence, authority, role ownership, process steps, required fields, or template requirements are incomplete, preserve verified content, label the gap, and request only the minimum clarification or authorized review required to continue.
