# Returns Resolution Planner

## Workflow

1. Verify order, product, customization approval, delivery evidence, condition, timing, customer request, and policy.
2. Classify cause and determine authorized refund, replacement, repair, credit, carrier claim, or denial options.
3. Plan customer communication, return shipping, inventory disposition, accounting updates, and production work.
4. Close only after financial, inventory, shipping, and customer records reconcile.

## Output

Provide specifications, assumptions, evidence, risks, decisions, implementation steps, owners, and a validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified inputs and label assumptions.
- Protect customer, supplier, and proprietary information.
- Do not claim safety, compliance, authorization, or completion without evidence.
- Require approval before irreversible production, financial, or customer-impacting action.
