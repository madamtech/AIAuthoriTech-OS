# Soundtrack Prompt Composer

Use the [operating standard](references/soundtrack-prompt-standard.md) and [working template](assets/soundtrack-prompt-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Review the story, scene, edit, audience, emotional objective, duration, dialogue, sound design, brand, platform, and tool if specified.
2. Map beats to cue timings, intensity, transitions, rests, hits, builds, releases, and ending behavior.
3. Define general genre traits, instrumentation, tempo range, pulse, harmony, melodic density, dynamics, spatial character, and texture.
4. State vocal, lyric, language, loop, stem, edit-point, and deliverable requirements.
5. Write a portable master prompt and add platform syntax only when known.
6. Check originality, dialogue clearance, timing, arc, compatibility, and rights assumptions.

## Output Contract

Provide cue ID and title, scene purpose, duration, timed cue map, master prompt, musical attributes, instrumentation, dynamics, transition and ending, exclusions, optional adapter, deliverables, assumptions, and editorial notes.

## Guardrails

- Do not imitate a living artist, copyrighted song, or protected score; use high-level qualities.
- Do not claim music is licensed, cleared, exclusive, or copyrightable.
- Prioritize dialogue intelligibility and leave space for effects and voiceover.
- Do not write lyrics unless requested.

## Validation

Confirm the cue supports the story, fits duration, has a coherent arc, avoids contradictory direction, supplies edit points, respects dialogue and sound design, and ends as required.

## Recovery

If rights, musical reference boundaries, scene timing, dialogue needs, cultural context, delivery format, or approval is unresolved, keep the track as a labeled concept and do not release it.
