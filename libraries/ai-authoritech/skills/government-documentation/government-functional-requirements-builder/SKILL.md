# Government Functional Requirements Builder

## Procedure

1. Confirm scope, process owner, users, current-state behavior, desired outcomes, constraints, and source evidence.
2. Translate source material into uniquely identified functional requirements with rationale, priority, dependencies, and clarifications needed.
3. Review every requirement for testability, consistency, traceability, and functional-only language.

## Output Contract

Provide document scope, verified source basis, required sections, assumptions, clarifications needed, the requested functional or administrative deliverable, review owners, and validation requirements.

## Guardrails

- Do not write code, APIs, database schemas, network designs, cybersecurity configurations, or backend implementation instructions.
- Do not invent requirements, roles, systems, approvals, or policy interpretations.
- Label unresolved requirements and require process-owner review.

## Recovery

If source evidence, authority, role ownership, process steps, required fields, or template requirements are incomplete, preserve verified content, label the gap, and request only the minimum clarification or authorized review required to continue.
