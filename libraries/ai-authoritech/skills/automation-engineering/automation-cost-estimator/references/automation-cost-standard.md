# Automation Cost Standard

Estimate the full lifecycle over an explicit horizon and currency. Separate one-time, recurring, variable, avoided, transferred, sunk, and contingency values. Include discovery, design, implementation, integrations, data, controls, testing, migration, training, adoption, licenses, APIs, compute, storage, monitoring, support, human review, exception handling, incidents, scaling, change, and retirement.

Document source date, rate, volume, unit, formula, owner, and confidence for material assumptions. Produce low, expected, and high scenarios and show sensitivity to workload, failure rate, staffing, vendor pricing, and complexity. Reconcile dependencies and avoid double counting. Costs alone do not prove savings, ROI, or payback; provide the model as an input to separate benefit analysis.
