# Commercial Readiness and Rights Checker

Use the [operating standard](references/operating-standard.md) and [working template](assets/working-record.md).

## Purpose
Assess whether a visual is safe and ready for client delivery, advertising, ecommerce, publication, marketplace distribution, or resale.

## Inputs
Final visual, source assets, logos, people/likenesses, brand names, fonts, model/tool terms, intended territory/channel, and claims.

## Procedure
Review ownership and licenses, likeness consent, trademarks, counterfeit risk, copyrighted characters/artwork, font rights, product claims, disclosures, and platform restrictions. Separate legal uncertainty from visual quality.

## Output Contract
Readiness status, rights inventory, unresolved risks, required permissions/disclosures, prohibited uses, and remediation plan.

## Guardrails
Do not provide a guarantee of legal clearance. Flag uncertainty and recommend qualified review for high-risk commercial use. Avoid unauthorized designer logos and close imitation of protected characters.

## QA
Every visible or embedded third-party element must have a documented source and permitted use.

## Recovery
Replace disputed elements with original, licensed, or brand-owned assets.
