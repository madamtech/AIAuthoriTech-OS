# ROI Calculator

Model transparent economics that decision-makers can challenge.

1. Confirm decision, currency, analysis horizon, comparison baseline, discount rate,
   tax treatment if supplied, and stakeholder-approved calculation policy.
2. Trace benefits to operational evidence from workflows, opportunity analysis, or
   measured pilots.
3. Separate benefits into capacity, avoided cost, revenue or margin, quality,
   risk reduction, and strategic option value. Prevent double counting.
4. Separate costs into discovery, design, data, integration, licenses and usage,
   infrastructure, security, governance, testing, change, training, support,
   monitoring, maintenance, contingency, and retirement.
5. Model benefit timing, ramp, adoption, utilization, leakage, and confidence.
6. Calculate with [references/business-case-method.md](references/business-case-method.md).
7. Produce conservative, base, and upside cases plus sensitivity and break-even
   analysis.
8. Distinguish cash savings, redeployable capacity, accounting impact, and
   nonfinancial value.
9. Reconcile every output to its inputs and label observed, approved, estimated,
   benchmarked, and unknown values.
10. Deliver with [assets/roi-business-case-template.md](assets/roi-business-case-template.md).

## Rules

- Do not turn hours saved into cash savings unless a credible realization mechanism
  exists.
- Do not count the same benefit in capacity, cost avoidance, and revenue.
- Do not omit ongoing model, vendor, control, monitoring, or change costs.
- Use ranges when volume, adoption, accuracy, cost, or timing is uncertain.
- Never recommend an investment solely because its ROI is positive; include risk,
  strategic fit, capacity, and alternatives.
- Require finance or accountable leadership approval for decision-grade assumptions.

## Handoff

Provide input ledger, scenarios, cash flows, ROI, payback, NPV when appropriate,
break-even thresholds, sensitivity drivers, nonfinancial benefits, exclusions,
confidence, and assumptions requiring approval.
