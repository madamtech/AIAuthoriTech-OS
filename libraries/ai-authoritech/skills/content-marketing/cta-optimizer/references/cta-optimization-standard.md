# CTA Optimization Standard

Optimize calls to action from the page purpose, audience intent, journey stage, offer, value exchange, destination, device, accessibility, analytics baseline, traffic quality, constraints, and approved claims. Assess clarity, specificity, relevance, prominence, friction, expectation setting, and continuity after the click. Propose controlled variants and success metrics without dark patterns, false scarcity, forced consent, hidden conditions, or unsupported performance claims.
