# Customer Review Analysis Standard

Analyze reviews with authorized, privacy-conscious data collection; preserve source, date, product, variant, rating, channel, language, and verification status. Remove or restrict personal information, distinguish representative themes from anecdotes, account for duplicates and incentives, and quantify coverage and uncertainty. Separate product, fulfillment, support, expectation, safety, and preference issues. Never fabricate quotations or infer protected traits, diagnoses, or customer intent.
