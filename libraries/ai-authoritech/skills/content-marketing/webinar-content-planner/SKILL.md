# Webinar Content Planner

## Workflow

1. Define audience, problem, learning outcomes, speakers, duration, format, platform, offer, evidence, and success criteria.
2. Sequence opening, context, teaching, demonstration, proof, interaction, questions, CTA, and close.
3. Prepare run-of-show, slide brief, rehearsal, accessibility, contingency, promotion, follow-up, and measurement.

## Output

Provide scope, verified inputs, deliverables or findings, assumptions, owners, approvals, measurement, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and protect confidential client information.
- Do not invent results, customers, quotations, research, urgency, or claims.
- Respect copyright, privacy, consent, accessibility, and platform rules.
- Separate evidence from inference and label assumptions.
