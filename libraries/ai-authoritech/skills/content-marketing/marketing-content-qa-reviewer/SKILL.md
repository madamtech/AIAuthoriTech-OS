# Marketing Content Qa Reviewer

Use the [operating standard](references/marketing-content-qa-standard.md) and [working template](assets/marketing-content-qa-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Confirm brief, source facts, audience, channel, brand rules, legal or compliance review needs, CTA, and acceptance criteria.
2. Check message hierarchy, proof, claims, grammar, terminology, links, metadata, visuals, alt text, mobile presentation, and cross-asset consistency.
3. Log severity, evidence, correction, owner, retest, and release recommendation.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, verified inputs, deliverables or findings, assumptions, owners, approvals, measurement, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and protect confidential client information.
- Do not invent results, customers, quotations, research, urgency, or claims.
- Respect copyright, privacy, consent, accessibility, and platform rules.
- Separate evidence from inference and label assumptions.

## Recovery

If the approved brief, source facts, claims evidence, rights, disclosure, technical preview, or approval authority is missing, return changes required and do not mark the asset ready to publish.
