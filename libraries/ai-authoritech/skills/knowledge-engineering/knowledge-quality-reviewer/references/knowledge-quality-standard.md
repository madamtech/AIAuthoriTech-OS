# Knowledge Quality Standard

Review an exact artifact version, population, evidence cutoff, risk class, and
consumer purpose. Verify provenance, authority, rights, consent, access class,
source identity, and immutable references before sampling content. Measure
accuracy, completeness, consistency, freshness, granularity, duplicates,
conflicts, metadata, findability, representativeness, accessibility, retention,
deletion, and citation integrity with explicit denominators and exclusions.
Apply hard gates to fabrication, provenance, privacy, access, and deletion
failures before weighted scores. Preserve reviewed evidence, record defects with
owners and retest scope, and limit the verdict to inspected versions and populations.
