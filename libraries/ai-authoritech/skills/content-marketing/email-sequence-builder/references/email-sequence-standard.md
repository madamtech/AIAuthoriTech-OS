# Email Sequence Standard

Design sequences from a verified trigger, lawful contact basis, lifecycle stage, audience, objective, sender, timing, frequency, branching, suppression, approved claims, personalization, CTA, accessibility, disclosure, unsubscribe, and measurement. Ensure every message has a purpose and coherent handoff. Test links, tokens, rendering, timezones, exits, and duplicate prevention. Never fabricate urgency, engagement, endorsements, or customer facts.
