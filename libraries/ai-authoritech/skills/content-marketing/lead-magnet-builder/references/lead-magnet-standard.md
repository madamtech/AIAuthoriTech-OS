# Lead Magnet Standard

Create from a specific audience problem, desired outcome, value promise, source evidence, scope, format, brand, rights, accessibility, CTA, form and consent context, delivery method, update owner, and measurement plan. Provide a useful standalone result without deceptive scarcity or bait-and-switch. Verify facts, examples, links, citations, privacy language, and downstream expectations, and define review and expiration for time-sensitive guidance.
