# Storyboard Direction Standard

Convert the locked screenplay and visual language into numbered panels that specify scene, shot, framing, camera height and angle, subject blocking, eyelines, movement, lens intent, lighting, duration, dialogue or action, transitions, continuity, and production notes. Maintain geography and screen direction, distinguish conceptual art from final capture, and respect rights and safety. Validate coverage, edit logic, feasibility, and handoffs to shot lists and generation prompts.
