# Fragrance Oil Safety Standard

Use the current IFRA Standards and supplier documentation for the exact fragrance
material and finished-product category. IFRA limits apply to the complete finished
product. Record certificate date, amendment, category, maximum use level, formula
use level, and reviewer.

Check essential oils individually for restrictions, oxidation, and photosensitivity.
Separate scent marketing from therapeutic aromatherapy claims. Use weight percent
for formula control and retain a batch and stability record.
