# Skincare Concept Planner

Use the [operating standard](references/skincare-concept-standard.md) and [working template](assets/skincare-concept-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define user, use case, format, price, texture, fragrance, exclusions, packaging, and desired experience.
2. Create a non-formula ingredient and benefit direction using verified supplier evidence.
3. Specify stability, compatibility, preservation, safety, claims, labeling, and regulatory reviews required before sale.

## Output Contract

Provide verified inputs, specifications, assumptions, risks, approvals, owners, implementation actions, and validation criteria.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and label assumptions.
- Protect customer, supplier, formula, and commercial information.
- Do not make unsupported cosmetic, health, safety, compliance, or financial claims.
- Require qualified review and authorization before regulated, production, pricing, or customer-impacting action.

## Recovery

If intended use, ingredient identity, supplier limits, allergens, preservation, stability, safety, or regulatory status is unresolved, label the work as a concept only. Do not provide production authorization, therapeutic claims, or a sellable formula.
