# Product Cost Calculator

Use the [operating standard](references/product-costing-standard.md) and [working template](assets/product-cost-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define unit, batch size, variants, currency, channel, tax treatment, and target margin.
2. Capture direct materials, waste, machine time, labor, design/setup, consumables, packaging, fees, shipping subsidy, overhead, rework, and failure allowance.
3. Separate one-time from recurring costs and fixed from variable costs.
4. Calculate unit cost, contribution margin, break-even, and scenario ranges.
5. Document assumptions and refresh triggers.

## Output Contract

Provide an assumptions table, cost breakdown, formulas, scenario comparison, price floor, target-price guidance, break-even, and sensitivity analysis.

## Guardrails

- Do not hide labor, waste, fees, or failed-production costs.
- Label estimates and uncertain inputs.
- Do not present tax or accounting treatment as professional advice.

## Recovery

If quantities, units, waste, labor, machine time, overhead, fees, shipping, discount, tax treatment, or target margin is unresolved, show scenarios and label assumptions. Do not publish a firm price until the owner approves the cost basis.
