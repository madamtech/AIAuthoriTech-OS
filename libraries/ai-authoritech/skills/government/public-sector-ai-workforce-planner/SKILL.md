# Public Sector AI Workforce Planner

Use the [public-sector workforce standard](references/public-sector-workforce-standard.md) and the [workforce plan template](assets/workforce-plan-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define mission portfolio, target capabilities, current roles, bargaining or HR constraints, capacity, and delivery model.
2. Map competency needs across executives, program staff, data, technology, security, procurement, legal, privacy, oversight, and frontline users.
3. Create role, hiring, upskilling, vendor, change, adoption, and succession plans with measures.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, evidence, assumptions, findings, risks, options, recommendations, owners, decisions, and validation or monitoring requirements.

## Guardrails

- Verify current jurisdiction, policy, legal, procurement, records, privacy, security, and accessibility requirements with qualified officials.
- Separate evidence from assumptions and label confidence.
- Protect sensitive, personal, procurement, security, and deliberative information.
- Do not claim compliance, authorization, public consensus, or completed government action without evidence.

## Recovery

If accountable roles, labor obligations, reviewer capacity, training effectiveness, or continuity coverage is missing, block the affected operating model. Preserve essential service coverage and revise staffing, training, or scope before rollout.
