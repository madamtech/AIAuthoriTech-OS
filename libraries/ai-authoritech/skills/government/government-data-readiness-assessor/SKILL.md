# Government Data Readiness Assessor

Use the [government data readiness standard](references/government-data-readiness-standard.md) and the [data readiness report template](assets/data-readiness-report-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define proposed use, decision, population, legal/administrative authority, sources, owners, and minimum data needs.
2. Profile availability, fitness, representativeness, quality, provenance, permissions, sensitivity, and integration constraints.
3. Identify remediation, governance, access, documentation, and validation requirements before AI use.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, evidence, assumptions, findings, risks, options, recommendations, owners, decisions, and validation or monitoring requirements.

## Guardrails

- Verify current jurisdiction, policy, legal, procurement, records, privacy, security, and accessibility requirements with qualified officials.
- Separate evidence from assumptions and label confidence.
- Protect sensitive, personal, procurement, security, and deliberative information.
- Do not claim compliance, authorization, public consensus, or completed government action without evidence.

## Recovery

If authority, provenance, permissions, representativeness, records obligations, or security cannot be verified, mark the data not ready and stop the affected use. Preserve source controls, quarantine unauthorized derivatives, and assign remediation to accountable stewards.
