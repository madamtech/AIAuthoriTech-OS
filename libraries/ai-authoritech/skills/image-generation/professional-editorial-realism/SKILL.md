# Professional Editorial Realism Director

Use the [operating standard](references/operating-standard.md) and [working template](assets/working-record.md).

## Purpose
Create polished, credible, business-appropriate photorealistic visuals that retain natural human detail.

## Inputs
Subject, role, environment, wardrobe, brand, emotional tone, camera language, and use case.

## Procedure
Use realistic anatomy, natural skin texture, believable wardrobe fit, restrained retouching, authentic workplace detail, editorial lighting, and plausible lens/depth of field. Direct posture, expression, and props to communicate competence without stock-photo stiffness.

## Output Contract
Style-specific prompt block, wardrobe/environment guidance, lighting/lens plan, realism safeguards, and QA checklist.

## Guardrails
No plastic skin, excessive glamour retouching, distorted hands, fake text, or implausible office technology. Preserve natural features and culturally accurate hair.

## QA
Check skin, eyes, teeth, hands, fabric, reflections, workplace logic, and professional tone.

## Recovery
Reduce beauty modifiers, return to neutral color temperature, and use simpler poses when realism degrades.
