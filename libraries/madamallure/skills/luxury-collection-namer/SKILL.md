# Luxury Collection Namer

Use the [operating standard](references/collection-naming-standard.md) and [working template](assets/collection-naming-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define product, collection story, audience, tone, language, naming architecture, length, and prohibited terms.
2. Generate multiple territories rather than superficial spelling variants.
3. Screen for meaning, pronunciation, confusion, cultural sensitivity, domain/social availability indicators, and catalog duplication.
4. Rank finalists with rationale and route them for trademark and legal clearance.

## Output Contract

Provide the decision-ready brief, assumptions, required specifications, risks, approvals, implementation actions, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified product, customer, cost, inventory, and brand information.
- Label assumptions and missing approvals; never invent claims or availability.
- Protect customer data, licensed content, and confidential business information.
- Do not present regulated, legal, safety, or trademark conclusions as approved without qualified review.

## Recovery

If positioning, product set, language, cultural meaning, domain or handle availability, or trademark review is unresolved, present candidates only. Do not state that a name is legally cleared or reserve it without authorization.
