# Business-case method

## Core formulas

- `net benefit = total realized benefits - total costs`
- `ROI % = net benefit / total costs × 100`
- `payback = first period cumulative net cash flow becomes nonnegative`
- `NPV = sum(cash flow at t / (1 + discount rate)^t)`
- `break-even adoption = costs / benefits at full adoption`, when the relationship
  is sufficiently linear

Do not calculate ROI when total costs are zero or materially incomplete.

## Realized benefit

For each benefit stream:

`realized benefit = gross potential × adoption × utilization × realization × confidence adjustment`

Use confidence adjustment only when the organization's method requires risk-adjusted
cash flow. Otherwise show confidence separately to avoid disguising assumptions.

## Scenario discipline

- Conservative: plausible adverse inputs, not an artificially bad case
- Base: best-supported expected inputs
- Upside: plausible favorable inputs, not the proposal target

Vary the few drivers that materially change the decision. Show unit, source,
owner, date, and confidence for every assumption.
