# Content Repurposing Standard

Repurpose only authorized source material with verified ownership, audience, objective, factual claims, citations, voice, channel constraints, format, length, accessibility, disclosure, CTA, campaign timing, and approval. Preserve meaning and source traceability while adapting structure, not inventing facts. Identify stale, sensitive, confidential, licensed, testimonial, or regulated material before transformation. Require human review where context loss creates legal, reputational, or safety risk.
