# Business Newsletter Builder

Use the [operating standard](references/business-newsletter-standard.md) and [working template](assets/business-newsletter-template.md).

## Procedure

1. Define audience, promise, frequency, sources, sections, owner, brand voice, CTA, consent, and goals.
2. Select timely themes and create subject, preheader, opening, insights, links, commentary, CTA, and accessible layout.
3. Verify facts and links, distinguish curation from endorsement, test rendering, and plan feedback and metrics.

## Output Contract

Provide scope, verified inputs, deliverables or findings, assumptions, owners, approvals, measurement, and validation checklist.

## Guardrails

- Use verified facts and protect confidential client information.
- Do not invent results, customers, quotations, research, urgency, or claims.
- Respect copyright, privacy, consent, accessibility, and platform rules.
- Separate evidence from inference and label assumptions.

## Recovery

If subscriber authority, suppression, source accuracy, link, disclosure, unsubscribe, schedule, or approval is unresolved, do not send the issue and correct the source package first.
